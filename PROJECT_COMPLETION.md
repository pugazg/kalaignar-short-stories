## Source-fidelity principles preserved

Throughout the completed work:

- the controlling scan remained the highest source authority;
- spelling, grammar, punctuation, sandhi, names and source anomalies were not silently modernized;
- older Tamil glyphs were resolved through full-span source review rather than contextual guessing;
- running headers, printed page numbers, printer signatures and other page furniture were excluded from story body text;
- suspicious but source-supported forms were retained in human-review queues rather than automatically corrected;
- English remained a separate, non-authoritative translation layer;
- translation did not overwrite or silently alter canonical Tamil;
- source PDFs, renders and crops were not committed to the repository.

## Preserved source-title differences

Two contents/story-heading differences remain intentionally preserved as source facts:

1. TOC `புரட்சிப்படம்` ↔ story-opening heading `புரட்சிப் படம்`;
2. TOC `சித்தார்த்தன்` ↔ story-opening heading `சித்தார்த்தன் சிலை`.

These must not be silently normalized in future work.
