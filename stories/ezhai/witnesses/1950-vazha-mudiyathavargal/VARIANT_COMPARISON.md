## High-value 1950 ↔ 1977 comparison

### Storm-image rewrite

The 1950 witness describes the frightening sky with **`பயங்கரக் கண்வீச்சு`**. The 1977 canonical uses **`பயங்கர மின்னீச்சு`**. Both belong to the same storm paragraph but are materially different figurative choices.

### Parvathi / Banu sentence

The 1950 witness gives a syntactically regular form equivalent to:

`பானு நனைந்து விடுவாளே என்ற பயம்தான்`

The 1977 source was explicitly reopened during the dual-gate audit and really does preserve the anomalous:

`பார்வதிக்கு பானு நனைந்து விடுவாளே என்று பயந்தான்.`

Therefore this is **not** a canonical-recheck candidate; it is strong evidence of edition-level rewriting.

### Dream transition

The 1950 witness has **`கனவு பங்கம் பாடுகிறது`**, whereas 1977 has **`கனவு மங்கலம் பாடுகிறது`**. This is retained as a genuine lexical edition variant.
