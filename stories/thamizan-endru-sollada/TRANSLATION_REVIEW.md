## Source-sensitive choices

- Verified forms `உள்ளத்தை பறிகொடுத்தவர்`, `தொகுப்பு நூலில்`, `குமரகுருபர்` / `குமரகுருபரை`, and `சொல்லுகிறார்` are translated from the corrected canonical Tamil and not reopened.
- Unusual `பெட்டி கலெக்டர்` is retained conservatively as **petti collector** rather than silently assigning an external administrative title.
- Joined `அந்ததமிழருக்கு` remains untouched in Tamil; English follows the sentence meaning without treating it as permission to normalize the source.
- The source’s final punctuation `சொல்லடா.. தலைநிமிர்ந்து நில்லடா...` is reflected as `Say you are a Tamil.. stand with your head held high...` rather than regularized to conventional punctuation.
- The names Stone, Kumaragurupar and Sambasivam are preserved from the verified Tamil without external biographical identification.
- No historical explanation of the colonial setting, Tamil movement or source family volume is inserted into the story body.
