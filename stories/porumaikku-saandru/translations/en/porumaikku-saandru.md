The Prophet Muhammad lived as a very ordinary, simple man. Though he was a great scholar and a great person, he was attached to a simple life. One day, whenever he walked along a particular street, a woman who disliked him would pour rubbish from the upper floor onto his head. Every day, when the Prophet came that way, rubbish would fall from above. He would shake it off and continue along his path.

One day, when he came by as usual, no rubbish was poured from that house. The Prophet was greatly surprised. He stopped in wonder and asked the people there, “Every day a lady pours rubbish on me—

where is she?” They said, “She is unwell. This stands as testimony to the Prophet’s patience, his loving heart and his steadfastness in the path of virtue.
