## Result

**SOURCE-VISIBLE COMPARISON CLOSED / PASS — 7/7 witness scans inspected.**

The surviving 1950 witness preserves the same core narrative architecture as the 1977 canonical: noisy theatre opening; bungled `திரெளபதி வஸ்திராபஹரணம்` performance; Kumar's instant infatuation with the Draupadi actor; collapse of the show into disorder and fire; Kumar pursuing the actor into the field; and the comic gender-reveal ending.

The 1950 version, however, preserves a **substantially longer final reveal** than the verified 1977 canonical. After the wig comes away, the earlier witness continues with the actor identifying himself as **கங்காதரன்**, discarding the Draupadi costume, and a final anti-`கண்டதும் காதல்` punchline. This is an edition-level ending expansion, not evidence that the 1977 canonical transcription omitted text.

## Highest-value edition findings

Notable source-supported differences include:

- 1950 uses internal **three-star scene dividers** in the opening sequence; the 1977 canonical presents the opening continuously;
- `நாடகத்துக்கு வந்தது` ↔ canonical `நாடகத்துக்கு வர்றது`;
- 1950 whistle / crowd-noise typography differs materially from 1977;
- 1950 `வாழ்க்கையிலே` ↔ canonical `வாழ்க்கையில்`;
- 1950 page composition and dialogue punctuation differ throughout;
- 1950 ending uses **`டோப்பா`** where 1977 has source-close **`‘டோபா’`**;
- after the wig reveal, 1950 continues with **`என் பெயர் கங்காதரன்`** and a costume-discard sequence absent from 1977;
- 1950 explicitly states that the Draupadi persona has disappeared and the trouser/dhoti-clad male actor remains;
- 1950 closes with an explicit **`கண்டதும் காதல் ஒழிக`** outcry;
- scan **36** closes with a large centered five-point star, not the 1977 short-rule / diamond ornament.
