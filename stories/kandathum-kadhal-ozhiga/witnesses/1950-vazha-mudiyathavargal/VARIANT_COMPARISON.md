## Authority and method

The attached 1950 `வாழமுடியாதவர்கள்` collection is an earlier comparison witness only. The verified 1977 first-edition anthology remains canonical.

## Earlier-edition ending expansion

The most important witness difference occurs on scan **36 / printed 35**.

The 1977 canonical closes immediately after the Draupadi actor removes the wig. The 1950 witness continues materially beyond that point. The surviving earlier text establishes that:

1. the actor identifies himself as **கங்காதரன்**;
2. the Draupadi costume is discarded;
3. the male actor, now out of the female stage persona, remains before Kumar;
4. the narration explicitly recasts the moment as a comic `திரெளபதி வஸ்திராபஹரணம்`;
5. Kumar's mind mocks his instant attraction;
6. the story closes with a `கண்டதும் காதல் ஒழிக` outcry.

This is a genuine edition-level ending expansion. It is not imported into the 1977 canonical Tamil or English.
