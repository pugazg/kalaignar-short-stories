A farmer has no water for his field and cannot raise a crop. He looks up at the sky in longing. There are no clouds in the sky. There is no rain either. Worried that rain may not come, he lies down and sleeps. The next morning it rains. He runs to the field. He ploughs. He cultivates the crop. He harvests it. Until he has ploughed, cultivated, harvested and brought the grain home and piled it up, to think that it was the cloud that gave him this rain—
