## Difficult / source-sensitive choices

- `சீமான் வீட்டு சீக்காளி` is rendered **“The Invalid in the Rich Man's House.”** `சீக்காளி` is kept as an intentionally human-sounding “invalid” rather than a title that would disclose the final animal reveal. `சீமான்` is rendered contextually as “rich man,” preserving the class contrast central to the story.
- The source-layer title distinction remains untouched: scan 41 opens `சீமான் வீட்டு சீக்காளி`, while later running headers use `சீமான் வீட்டுச் சீக்காளி!`.
- The opening onomatopoeia `பொசக்`, the crying sound `கோர்`, and the later baby/dog sounds are carried into English conservatively rather than replaced with invented explanations.
- Scan 42 source-odd `—,மெட்ரா’சிலேருந்தா?...` is translated as a trunk call “from Madras” while the unusual Tamil punctuation/spelling remains unchanged in the canonical source.
- Scan 43 `முடியும்ணு` is translated by its colloquial contextual sense without altering the verified Tamil form.
- Scan 43 source-odd `செதாஸ் கோப்` is retained in English as **“sethascope”** rather than silently normalizing the Tamil to a different spelling. The surrounding action—one car carrying the doctor and the other the instrument—is preserved because it participates in the satire.
- Scan 43 truncated-looking but verified `தங்கியிருக்க வேண்;` is rendered contextually as “You must stay right here;” in English only. No Tamil completion was inserted into the canonical text.
- Scan 46 verified source-odd `பாரத்தைப் போட்டு` is translated minimally as **“put together a load”** rather than being silently repaired into a guessed vehicle or stretcher term.
- Scan 47 `எதிர் கொண்டழைத்தாள்` is rendered as the nurse **receiving him and leading him in**, a conservative contextual translation; the Tamil is not normalized.
- Scan 48 source `——` punctuation after `“யாருக்கு? என்ன விஷயம்?”` is retained as a double dash in the English derivative.
- Scan 49 `வள்! வள்!` is retained as **“Val! Val!”** inside an explicitly described bark, preserving the source sound while allowing the final reveal to function.
- `பாட்டாளியின் மகள்` is rendered **“the labourer’s daughter”**, maintaining the explicit class opposition between the rich household and the working family.
