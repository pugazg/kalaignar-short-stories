# Prison Is Cruel

<!-- source scan 28; printed page 27 -->

Stick a chilli fruit into the head of a tender bottle-gourd, place jasmine leaves in the middle and a screw-pine petal behind as wings — and there you have a green parrot!

A creation like you! A creation like you!
O round head! O red beak!
O little wing! O long tail!
Lovely-eyed parrot, what I said was wrong!
When asked of Vannan, you forget to praise him.
Even when told to praise, you will not extol!
I know the reason, little parrot!
Because I put you in a cage

<!-- source scan 29; printed page 28 -->

you too writhe like a worm on a hook!
I rejoiced in shutting you in prison.
Today I understand.
Prison is very cruel! Prison is very cruel!
