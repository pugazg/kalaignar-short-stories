# The Tiger Was the One to Be Killed, But...

<!-- source scan 16; printed page 15 -->

...Once, Valvil Ori was the ruler of the Kolli Hills. He was one of the Last Seven Great Patrons. The poet Vanparanar comes seeking a gift from that Ori. As he comes, he composes a song about Ori. Do you know the meaning of that song?

“King Valvil Ori! The arrow in your hand is an arrow capable of bringing down an elephant. With such an arrow, a tiger is what you

<!-- source scan 17; printed page 16 -->

shot. That arrow struck the tiger, drank its life — then left the tiger, struck a deer that was running there, pierced its body, struck the body of a boar roaming there and killed that boar too, and finally lodged in the body of a monitor lizard lying in an anthill.” Thus he speaks of the greatness of that arrow.

The tiger was the one that had to be killed. But it is when the deer too was killed along with it that we feel sorrow.
