At a house of mourning, a woman who had come to sing an oppari lament cried:

“Bitter gourd on the pandal,
Bitter gourd on the pandal,
It is hanging there, sister!”

Another woman is said to have sung:

“We can look at it when we leave,
We can look at it when we leave.”

At once the woman of the house sang:

“Ayyo, I have left that one there for seed,
I have left it there for seed.”
