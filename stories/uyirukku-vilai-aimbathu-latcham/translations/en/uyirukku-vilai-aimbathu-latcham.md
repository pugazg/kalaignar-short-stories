# Fifty Lakhs — The Price of a Life

Mr. Lanjayya barks, “Who is that?”

“It is I, Yama-Dharma.”

“Where is your buffalo vehicle?”

“I have lent it to the government departments.”

“What happened to their tortoise vehicle?”

“It is under repair.”

“The snail vehicle too?”

“That one has been sent to the city councils for their water service!”

<!-- source scan 43; printed page 42 -->

“Oh! So the departments keep the buffalo and the snail, but when it comes to colliding with people in the streets they keep deer vehicles and rabbit vehicles?”

“Leave that aside. Come, let us go.”

“Where?”

“Do you still not understand? You are dead. Come, let us go to Yamaloka.”

“Yamaloka? Are there Panchayat Unions, municipalities, corporations, a Legislative Assembly, an Upper House, Parliament, Rajya Sabha and so on there? Are there parties? Are there elections?”

“Not a single nuisance. Just one peaceful world!”

‘Tch, tch. I do not like peaceful places at all. For my affairs to get done there must be plenty of commotion and confusion... I am not coming with you.”

“Mr. Lanjayya! You must not say that. Otherwise I will be stained with the charge that I failed in my duty.”

“Hey, Yama-Dharma! Do you know the one way to keep that stain from falling on you?.... If I am to come with you, you must give me a cheque for five lakh rupees right now.”

Yama-Dharma’s head reeled. “A bribe even for dying?” he muttered.

“.... If I am to be born in the next birth, I will ask for that too, you know....”

<!-- source scan 44; printed page 43 -->

“.... Do not take another birth. Ask for that too right now and give me a little ‘concession’.”

“.... Give me ten lakhs.”

“.... This fellow can only be beaten to death with money,” Yama says, taking out a cheque and writing it. He holds out the cheque.

“Here—die by cash! You, die by cash!”

Mr. Lanjayya looks at the cheque. “Fifty lakhs.”

“Ah! Fifty lakhs!” Mr. Lanjayya’s mouth falls open.

After that, he never closes it again.
