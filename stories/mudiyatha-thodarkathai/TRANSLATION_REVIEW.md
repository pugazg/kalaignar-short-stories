## Difficult / source-sensitive choices

- `முடியாத தொடர்கதை` is rendered **“The Never-Ending Story.”** The recurring `காதலோர் தொடர்கதையே` / `காதல் தொடர்கதை` idea is rendered as **“Love is a continuing tale”** so the title's sense of something that cannot end remains visible without treating the English title as a replacement for the Tamil.
- The source scene numbering visible in the verified Tamil is **1 → 2 → 4 → 5**. English preserves that sequence exactly and does not invent a missing `3`.
- The colloquial Sangu/Sandhanam dialogue is made readable in English, but no Tamil form is normalized or rewritten in the source layer.
- Scan 61 source-odd `ஆடுதன் ராஜா` is retained as **“Aaduthan Raja”** rather than being identified from outside knowledge.
- `விவேக சிந்தாமணி` and `விரக்தாப சிந்தாமணி` are retained as **Viveka Chintamani** and **Virakthaba Chintamani**; no external literary identification is added.
- Scan 68 source-odd `களிவற்ற கொளியும்` is rendered contextually as a **“joyless tune”** in English only; the verified Tamil remains untouched.
- Scan 74's verified sequence `அவலட்சணத்துக்கும் அறிவுக் குந்தானடா ... அதிலே அழகு...` is internally unusual. English keeps the printed contrast as **“between ugliness and intelligence”** rather than silently repairing the Tamil to an expected wording.
- Scan 75 `மீனவி புதல்வன்` remains opaque; English retains **“Meenavi's son”** rather than inventing an identification.
- The Bharathi lines are translated only to the extent quoted inside the verified story. No external version, correction or additional verse is imported.
- The Valli/Murugan/Deivayanai discussion is translated as stated by the characters; no external mythological correction or explanatory addition is introduced.
- The satirical `கோழிகடி சாமியார்` / `குஞ்சுக்கடி சாமியார்` names are rendered literally as **Chicken-Bite Swami / Chick-Bite Swami**, preserving the story's comic mechanism without supplying an external identity.
- Scan 90 contains the verified, source-odd kinship sequence `தந்தையை அண்ணை-இழந்து, தனயனையும் ... தனயன் மனைவியையும்`. The English follows the surrounding story referents conservatively as loss of Kumudham's father, brother and the brother's Burmese wife, because the same verified span immediately identifies Chitra as the Burmese woman's child and scan 91 explicitly calls Chitra `என் அண்ணன் மகள்`. This interpretive choice is documented here; the canonical Tamil is not changed or silently reconciled.
- The corrected source readings `துட்ட லக்கணமாம்`, `மலைப்பாம்பு`, `பில்கனாவிலே`, `புகுந்துவிட்டவளை`, `பேசினாள்`, `சொல்லியனுப்பினான்`, `விலக்கினான்`, and `கூறினான்` are followed as verified.
- Source-faithful `ஸ்பரிசிக்கப்பட்டு` is rendered by its contextual sense **“touched”**; scan-90 `தந்தையை அண்ணை-இழந்து` remains explicitly documented rather than normalized in Tamil.
- The final mistaken-sibling inference is preserved exactly as the plot presents it: Chitra and Selvam act on the incomplete first-page reading of the diary, while the later fuller account identifies Chitra as Kumudham's niece. The English does not pre-empt or soften that tragic misunderstanding.
