- different comma / semicolon / dash / ellipsis density;
- quotation-mark and display-emphasis differences;
- `கோயில்` ↔ `கோவில்`;

## Canonical-recheck review

No 1950 difference was promoted to a canonical-recheck candidate.

In particular, the 1950 forms:

- `அலைகழியும்`,
- `கட்டினன்`,
- `என்ன கம்பாசிட்டர்! ஆச்சா?`

look more syntactically ordinary than the current 1977 canonical forms, but the exact 1977 source pages were already directly checked during the 2026 dual-gate audit and those unusual forms were deliberately retained. The witness therefore demonstrates edition revision rather than a canonical transcription defect.
