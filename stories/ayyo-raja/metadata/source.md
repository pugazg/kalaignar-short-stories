## Source-review notes

All Story 31 pages were reviewed directly from the native **3146×4826** embedded scan images. Enlarged nearest-neighbour/full-span crops were used where older Tamil glyph forms or source-close wording could be misread. The controlling scan governs the transcription; contextual expectation was not substituted for the printed form.

A specific old-glyph correction was made on scan **217**: the speech tag is **`என்றாள் முத்தம்மா`**. The older `றா` form can visually resemble a different vowel construction in this typeface, so the earlier provisional `என்றுள்` reading was rejected after source reinspection and user correction.
