## Source-review method

All seven story scans were directly reviewed from native **3146×4826** embedded page images. Enlarged crops and nearest-neighbour/full-span checks were used for older Tamil glyph forms, unusual wording, punctuation and physical joins. Scan **218** was independently inspected as the Story 32 boundary witness. The controlling scan, not contextual expectation, determined the readings.

A previous provisional reading on scan **217** was explicitly reopened after user correction: `என்றுள்` was wrong. Reinspection of the old Tamil typeface confirms **`என்றாள் முத்தம்மா`**. The older `றா` glyph caused the misread, and the corrected reading is propagated through the page record, assembly, source metadata, review queue and this audit.
