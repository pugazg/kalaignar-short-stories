## Relation to 1977 source-proven repairs

Several 1977 readings that required 2026 direct-source repair differ from the 1953 witness:

- 1953 `பார்க்கவில்லை` vs repaired 1977 `பார்க்க வில்லை`;
- 1953 `அழுதிடும்` vs repaired 1977 `அழுதிடுங்`;
- 1953 `பார்த்திருந்தாலும்` vs repaired 1977 `பார்த்திருந்தாலுங்`.

This independently demonstrates that the two editions were not mechanically identical. It does **not** reopen the 1977 repairs.

## Structural / layout variants

### Opening

1953 scan **16 / printed 14** displays the heading `சபலம்` without the long horizontal rule represented in the 1977 source.

### Early internal divider

1953 uses a centered row of **three open circles** before the family-introduction paragraph beginning with Kumares / Chandra.

The 1977 source does not use that divider at the corresponding point.

### `வண்டி...` transition

The 1977 source uses an enlarged initial `வ` for the later `வண்டி நெல்லிக்குப்பத்தைத் தாண்டிவிட்டது` paragraph. The 1953 source uses ordinary running type.

### Closing

1953 scan **23 / printed 21** ends after the departing-train sentence with blank lower-page space and no visible 1977-style closing ornament.

## Canonical-recheck review

No 1953 difference was promoted to a canonical-recheck candidate.

The 1977 source was independently reopened at native resolution and its unusual spacing / sandhi forms were directly established. The 1953 forms are therefore retained as edition-history evidence only.
