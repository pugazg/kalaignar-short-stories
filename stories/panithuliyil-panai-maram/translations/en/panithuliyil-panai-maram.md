A poet is standing in a meadow in the morning. Because dew has fallen there, a dewdrop clings to the tip of a blade of grass. Beside where the poet is standing there is a

palm tree. The entire form of that palm tree is reflected in the dewdrop on the tip of the grass. The poet says: “Just as this whole palm tree is reflected in the dewdrop at the tip of the grass, so too all the ideas in the world are reflected in that dewdrop called the Tirukkural.” (Loud applause) Thus the poet describes it very beautifully.
