## Source-sensitive choices

- differing source forms `அறிவிற் குறைந்த` and `அறிவிலிக் கணவன்` are both translated contextually as lacking sense / foolish husband; this does not normalize the Tamil source forms.
- the comic **bull → goat → hen → tea** sequence is translated exactly in that order without rationalizing the exchanges.
- unusual `தேநீரையாவது வயிறு நிரம்பச் சாப்பிட்டீர்களா?` is rendered conservatively as **“At least did you have enough of that tea to fill your stomach?”**; the source wording remains documented in the Tamil/control layer.
- corrected scan-42 `என்னடா?` is represented as **“What, man?”**; the rejected earlier reading is not reintroduced.
- source-close `அப்படி யொன்றுமில்லை` is translated for meaning without altering the Tamil spacing.
- `பெட்டிக்கடை` is rendered as **small shop** rather than assigned a more specific retail category not supplied by the source.
