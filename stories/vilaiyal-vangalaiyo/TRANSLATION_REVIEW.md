## Difficult / conservative renderings

- `மூஞ்சிக்கிஞ்சி கழுவி` is rendered naturally as **“wash your face”** without inventing additional action.
- `வக்கணை பேசாதே` is rendered **“Don't talk back”**, preserving the scolding force without adding a more specific accusation.
- `வாட்டுப் பயல்` is rendered **“that wretched fellow”**; this remains a conservative colloquial rendering.
- `அவன் காலை வாங்கி விடுவார்` is rendered **“the zamindar will have his leg broken”**, preserving the threat rather than translating the idiom mechanically word-for-word.
- `நஞ்சாவது பஞ்சாவது` is rendered **“Poison or famine...”** so the source's dismissive paired phrasing remains visible.
- `கம்பி எண்ணணும்` is rendered **“you'll be counting prison bars”**, preserving the source idiom.
- `என்னை முதல் திருமணம் செய்துகொள்ள` is rendered minimally as **“to marry me first.”** No external interpretation of the phrase has been inserted.
- `என் உபதேசங்கள் எல்லாம் அவருக்கு ஊமையின் பேச்சாகத் தெரிந்தன` is rendered close to the source as **“All my admonitions were like the speech of a mute to him.”**
- `வீரலக்ஷ்மி` is rendered **“brave Lakshmi”** in the draft; final terminology review will revisit whether a transliterated `Veera-Lakshmi` better preserves the rhetorical form.
