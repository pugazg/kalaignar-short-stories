# Five Husbands in the Next Birth

“Nala! Why do you keep crying like this all the time?”

“Why ask me the reason, Idhaya! Don’t you know that I am writhing like a worm in embers? Do you too hear the music of those birds calling in pairs? Don’t you know what pangs of separation the veena music played by lovers from the grove can awaken in a young woman? You know! You know it, yet you keep silent like this merely to console me—isn’t that so?”

“It is true, Nalayini! Ulaga praised you as a chaste wife. I heard it too, standing hidden. You ought to be satisfied that the light of that fame alone is enough.”

“Idhaya! Tell me the truth. Am I a chaste wife? How? How?”

“Yes! You sent your leprosy-stricken husband to a courtesan’s house!”

“Don’t mock me, Idhaya! I have no desire to lie with a man afflicted by leprosy. He calls me to him with desire. My hand trembles at the thought of embracing him. That is why I sent him to the courtesan’s house. I thought it would be fine if his longing were satisfied without danger to my beauty. Don’t you know this trick, Idhaya!”

“Is there anything I do not know, Nalayini? Yet Ulaga believes in your chastity! She thinks that carrying the leper on your head to a courtesan’s house was an act of great devotion to your husband.”

“You too say that I carried him on my head! Correct yourself. I put him in a basket and carried the basket on my head. Am I mad enough to touch a leper with my hands and carry him on my head? I was careful to put him in a basket and take him that way!”

“Nala! Even when your husband spoke however harshly, you never answered him back. Just yesterday Ulaga praised you to me as a queen of chastity who would not speak a word in reply.”

“My husband scolded me. I did not answer. That is true! I ignored it, thinking, after all, it is only a leper scolding me. If I spoke to him too, the talk would grow. Because of that argument I would have to stand close to him and exchange words. That is why, dear, I simply let that leprosy-stricken fellow go on scolding.”

“Hmm. Somehow you have won the title of chaste wife. Ulaga has praised you as a devoted wife whose chastity is acclaimed by the world, as a woman full of devotion to her husband!”

“What does Ulaga know, dear, about the volcano inside my heart? Is the title of chaste wife enough for this pitiable woman whose youth is burning away? Tell me, Idhaya, tell me! Show me a way to a life of pleasure!”

“I will show you the way, Nalayini. I shall give you a new boon. Bring this birth to an end quickly and take another birth soon! In that birth you are going to be born as Draupadi! Though you have one husband now, you have no excitement, no enjoyment; your heart is full of pain! So in your next birth, live a joyful life with five husbands. I give you this wonderful boon.”
