Once Drona called all the students studying under him, pointed to a tree and said, “A parrot is sitting on that tree. You must aim at the parrot, shoot an arrow and cut its neck.” He then called Duryodhana and

the other students, showed them the tree and the parrot, and asked each one in turn, “What do you see?” All of them said, “I see the tree; I see the branch.” He dismissed them all as not being right. He called the young Arjuna and asked, “What do you see?” Arjuna said, “I see nothing else. I see only the tip of the arrow and the parrot's head.” Of the parrot that Gandiban intended to bring down, he saw only its head and the tip of the arrow. The story says that Drona patted him in approval and said, “You are the one skilled at taking aim, the one capable of victory.”
