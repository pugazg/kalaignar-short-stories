# How Did They Reach Heaven?

<!-- source scan 19; printed page 18 -->

In the world of heaven... grove, feast!

Ignoring all these, a group sat on the grass. A yellow strip of cloth hung from their shoulders. On it, in blue, was written “Immigrant.” From their faces it was quite clear that they were inhabitants of the earthly world. Yes... earth-dwellers indeed! Kannappa Nayanar, Karaikkal Ammaiyar, Nandanar, Siruthondar... all the others were there too. Their conversation did not become a great debate. Each spoke a word. It was about the way they had come to the heavenly world!

“I received God's grace only after gouging out both my eyes.”

— Kannappa Nayanar

“I cut up my child, cooked the flesh, and my wife and I pulled off its head, ground it into chutney and served a meal. Only after that did Sivaperuman relent and take me under his grace.”

— Siruthondar

“‘Immerse yourself in fire, burn away all impurity and come with a golden body,’ the compassionate one told the Dikshitar in a dream. Only after I made that sacrifice did I receive darshan of the sacred feet of Thillaiyappan.”

— Nandanar

<!-- source scan 20; printed page 19 -->

“I tore away all the flesh on my body, drained away the blood, and the glorious lord told me to come as a skeleton — like a ghost — walking upside down! Only after undergoing that hardship did Kailasapathi bestow his grace.”

— Karaikkal Ammaiyar

“I cut grass in temples; I carried the palanquin of the Brahmin Thirugnanasambandar. Only after doing all that was I permitted to come to the world of the immortals!”

— Appar

“For a human being, honour is precious; I pawned that honour and sent my wife to the bedchamber with Paramasivan. As the reward for that sacrifice, Paramar granted me this life in the supreme realm.”

— Iyarpagai Nayanar

“I rubbed my elbow in place of the sandalwood block and performed worship of the Lord. The result of that is this heavenly world for me.”

— Moorthi Nayanar

“Ah! Do you know how many people I killed to come to this world? I struck down with an axe and killed all those who reviled Siva. Only after showing all those corpses to the mad one who wears the crescent did I receive this reward!”

— Eripatha Nayanar

“Because they ate the paddy that had been set aside for Siva during the famine... because of devotion, I killed my mother, father, my beloved wife and... because the mother who had eaten the paddy...

<!-- source scan 21; printed page 20 -->

...would have fed milk to my beloved child, I slashed them all to death with a curved sword. Only afterward did the red-matted-haired one adorned with konrai forgive my crime and appear before me.”

— Kotpuli Nayanar

Thus the devotees spoke of their sacrifices with pride. They heaved deep sighs.

Then, hearing a sudden voice, Kannappar, who had opened his mouth to speak, stopped. Everyone turned and looked. A sacred-thread-wearing Iyer stood with his arm around the shoulder of a woman whose youth had faded. All the eminent devotees stared at his mouth. The Iyer spoke:

“You all boast that you made great, great sacrifices to come to the world of moksha; when I think of it, I feel like laughing.

“You say you endured unspeakable troubles to come to heaven. Look here: I have come to Sivaloka without any difficulty at all.

(The devotees were stunned with amazement.)

“Do not be surprised, friends! Here, leaning on me, is my mother.

(Kotpuli Nayanar interrupts) “Your mother?”

“Yes, my mother! The mother who carried me for ten months and gave birth to me. The mother who placed me in a beautiful cradle and sang *aararo*. I have taken this mother as my wife. Not only here; even in the earthly world this mother was my beloved mistress.

<!-- source scan 22; printed page 21 -->

“She rocked me in a cradle. I gave her pleasure in bed. You may say that making one's mother one's wife is a great sin. As an even greater sin, I immediately killed my father when he saw us together on the bed. You ask, did heaven's gate open for you? Yes; heaven yielded easily to me. I cried, ‘Araga Mahadeva!’ With Mother in my embrace, Father, in *Ridaba-vahana* form, showered grace. I came joyfully to the land of heaven.”

The Nayanmars were stunned. Karaikkal Ammaiyar said, “Is this true?”

“Still doubt? If you have doubts, look at the *Mapathakam Theertha Padalam* of the *Thiruvilaiyadal Puranam*,” said the Iyer.

“What is the reason that a sinner like you alone obtained life in heaven so easily?” asked Siruthondar.

“This is the reason,” said the Iyer, pulling out and showing his sacred thread.
