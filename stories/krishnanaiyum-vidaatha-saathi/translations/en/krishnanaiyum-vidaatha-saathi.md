# Caste Did Not Spare Even Krishna

<!-- source scan 41; printed page 40 -->

They tell a story even about Krishna, the god of the Yadavas. Myth says that he was a man of great ability, a great statesman and so on. But he belonged to the Yadava class. Therefore, they said that only Kshatriyas could rule a country and declared that he had no right to rule.

That is why, the story says, Krishna, though born in Uttar Pradesh, could not establish a kingdom here and went to Gujarat, where he founded a new kingdom called ‘Dwaraka’. Thus caste difference did not spare even the avatar called Krishna.
