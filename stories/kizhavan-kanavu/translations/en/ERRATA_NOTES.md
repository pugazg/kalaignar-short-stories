## Policy

Scan **23** contains the publication's printed **`பிழை திருத்தம்.`** table. It is treated as a separate publisher-supplied correction layer.

The archival English translation follows the finalized visible page readings from scans **7–22**. The publisher's corrections are therefore **not silently substituted** into `kizhavan-kanavu-en.md` or the batch translations.

If a future normalized/reading edition chooses to apply these corrections, that edition must identify them explicitly as publisher-errata-derived changes.

## Important translation distinction — scan 13 / printed page 9

The archival page visibly reads:

- `வைத்திருந்தான்`

The publisher's scan-23 errata says:

- `வைத்திருந்தாள்`

The English sentence in Batch 2 reads **“This alone was what Rajam had kept in reserve for Mallika.”** English does not encode the Tamil masculine/feminine distinction in that verb form. The translation therefore does not visibly reveal the difference, but editorially the two Tamil source layers remain distinct:

1. archival page reading — `வைத்திருந்தான்`;
2. publisher errata — `வைத்திருந்தாள்`.

This is not treated as permission to replace the archival Tamil layer silently.

## Other errata entries

For the remaining nine corrections, this English errata file records only what scan 23 itself supplies as the correction. It does **not** invent or reconstruct the underlying erroneous printed form unless that form is independently established by the archival page record.

Where the English translation naturally corresponds to the publisher's corrected sense, that does not mean the errata was silently applied; the controlling translation basis remains the finalized archival page record.

## Release rule

Any release report for this English translation must state that:

- scan 23 contains **10** publisher corrections;
- the errata remains separately documented;
- the archival English prose was not silently normalized from the errata table;
- the scan-13 `வைத்திருந்தான்` / `வைத்திருந்தாள்` distinction remains explicitly documented.
