### Errata policy

The errata is evidence from the same physical publication, but it is a separate textual layer. It must **not silently overwrite** the page-level transcription of what is visibly printed on the story pages.

A later assembled reading text may expose two layers explicitly:

1. archival page reading — exactly what the page prints;
2. documented errata reading — the publisher's printed correction from scan 23.
