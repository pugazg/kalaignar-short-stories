# Variant comparison — சங்கிலிச்சாமி — 1977 canonical ↔ 2009 witness

## Coverage

All 2009 scans **111–125** were inspected from the source images. Scan **126** was checked only as the `தப்பிவிட்டார்கள்` boundary.

## Finding

Same story structure and conclusion; no 2009-only narrative section. The later edition uses revised typography, spacing and selected word forms.

Representative evidence:

- canonical recheck `நமப்பார்வதி படே` ↔ 2009 `நமப்பார்வதி பதே` — high-priority controlling-scan recheck candidate;
- opening devotional compounds are more regularly joined/spaced in 2009;
- dialogue punctuation and colloquial spelling are substantially regularized;
- the unusual `ஸ்வாமி நாளைக் காலையில்...` passage is not eliminated by the later witness and therefore cannot be normalized merely from context;
- the narrative still closes with the satire on the profitable `மடம்` and public credulity, followed by six ornaments.

The 2009 witness also confirms the collection-level pagination anomaly: `சங்கிலிச்சாமி` physically begins on scan 111 after `குப்பைத் தொட்டி` closes on scan 110/p105.

Canonical 1977 Tamil and English remain unchanged pending direct reopening of any disputed 1977 scan.
