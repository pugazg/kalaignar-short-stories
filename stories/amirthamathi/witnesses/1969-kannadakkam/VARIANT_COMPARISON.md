# Variant comparison — அமிர்தமதி — 1969 witness ↔ 1977 canonical

## Authority and method

The attached **1969 second edition** is an independent comparison witness only. The verified **1977 first-edition anthology** remains canonical.

The complete 1969 witness, scans **32–41 / printed 53–62**, was compared end-to-end against the final 1977 canonical assembly, page records and 2026 dual-gate ledger. Historical Tamil glyphs were read by character identity, not by modern visual resemblance.

No OCR, external website, or substitute edition controls this comparison.

## Structural finding

**Equivalent narrative / no structural expansion.**

Both editions contain:

1. the literary-theft complaint frame;
2. the `சுகம் எங்கே?` / `யசோதர காவியம்` setup;
3. Amirthamathi's privileged royal setting;
4. the night-song and elephant-keeper discovery;
5. her relationship with the keeper;
6. the king's discovery and poisoning outcome;
7. the analogy back to literary theft;
8. the same final refusal to name the alleged thieves.

No major block is unique to either edition.
