# Grass on a Coconut Tree

> **English translation of the verified Tamil archival reading.** Controlling source: 1987 collection `கலைஞர் சொன்ன குட்டிக் கதைகள்`, lower scan **7 / printed page 6**. The verified Tamil remains authoritative.

<!-- anthology scan: 7; printed page: 6; lower shared span -->

To deceive the people, all the sons of M.G.R.'s elder brother, M. G. Chakrapani, have said, “We did not take a bribe to grant a licence for an arrack factory. We borrowed ten and three-quarter lakh rupees as a loan to make a cinema film.”

At midnight, when no one was around, a thief climbed a coconut tree in a coconut grove—to pluck bunches of coconuts! The owner of the grove saw him.

“Come down,” he said. The thief came down. The owner gave him a slap and asked why he had climbed the coconut tree. The thief said he had climbed it to pull out grass. “What grass is there on a coconut tree?” the grove owner asked. “That is exactly what I checked. There was no grass, so I came down,” the thief said.

Like the story of pulling grass from a coconut tree, they have said that they merely borrowed money to make a cinema film and did not take it as a bribe.

## Translation note

- The complete story occupies only the lower shared span of scan 7 / printed page 6.
- `சாராயத் தொழிற்சாலை` is rendered “arrack factory”; no broader outside identification has been added.
- No verified Tamil wording was changed during translation.
