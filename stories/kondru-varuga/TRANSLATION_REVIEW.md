## E2 — meaning-fidelity review

**PASS — sentence-by-sentence semantic review complete.**

Eight meaning-fidelity repairs/refinements were made:

1. A loose “Where did this anklet come from?” was refined to **“How did you come by this anklet?”** for உனக்கேது இந்தச் சிலம்பு?.
