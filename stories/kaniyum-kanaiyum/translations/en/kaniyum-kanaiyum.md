# The Fruit and the Arrow

At a public meeting held in Chennai's 66th ward, I explained it this way.

“.... In an English story, an archer named William Tell faced a test. He had to shoot an arrow and split an apple placed on his child's head. If the thought that his child was beneath the fruit had entered the warrior's mind, his bow would have trembled. The bowstring would have trembled—the arrow would have trembled. The child would have died.

But the warrior, with the single aim of splitting the apple, shoots the arrow—the fruit is split—without any harm coming to the child.

“Here, Republic Day is the child! The poisonous fruit on its head is Hindi; the DMK is a party skilled at taking aim. Without bringing dishonour to Republic Day, the DMK has the skill to shoot its arrow and succeed without failing to split Hindi imperialism as well. I say this to those virtuous people who claim that the DMK is insulting Republic Day and thereby provoke and incite others.
