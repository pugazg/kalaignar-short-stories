# In Those Days!

<!-- source scan 17; printed page 16 -->

In those days Bharata, the younger brother, bore on his head the sandals of Rama, born to rule, placed them upon the royal throne and ruled as their representative — was he not the true younger brother...

...In those same days when Bharata, who bore his elder brother's sandals and stood as an example of brotherly affection, lived, there was also Vibhishana — the brother who betrayed his own elder brother Ravana to Bharata's elder brother, the Ayodhyan...

Students these days place thorns on the teacher's chair. In those days there was Ekalavya, who cut off his thumb and offered it as an oblation to his guru Dronacharya...

....In those very days when Ekalavya, the foremost disciple who gave his thumb to Acharya Drona, lived — there was also Chandra, who consorted with Tara, the guru's wife...
