# I Will Lift the Mountain!

“I will carry a mountain on my head and parade through the whole country. That is my first plan. I will bend the sky into a bow and twist sand into a rope to serve as a string worthy of that bow. That is my second plan. I have many more wondrous plans one after another. Later I shall carry them out before the people of this country.”

Thus, in the middle of a public assembly, a young man slapped his shoulders and issued his challenge. The people listened open-mouthed to his words and were spellbound. News that such a young man was going about speaking this way reached the king’s ears. The king ordered that he be brought to the royal hall. When the youth entered the hall, the king did not dismiss him lightly but asked whether the plans he had announced to the people were really possible.

“There is nothing impossible in this world, Your Majesty! I will certainly accomplish the plans I have announced,” the young man proclaimed, standing erect.

“If that is so, brave young man, carry out your first plan before me. Are you ready?”

To this question, brimming with the king’s eagerness, he replied very calmly.

“O king of kings! Does not every undertaking require certain facilities and opportunities? I shall certainly accomplish what I told the people of the country and what I declared before your august presence. But there is one thing. I have travelled for many days and am exhausted. If it pleases you, I ask that you receive me as a guest of the palace for a month or two. Once my body has regained its strength, I shall carry out my first plan—lifting the mountain on my head.”

The king happily accepted his request. “Why only a month or two? Take a full three months. During those three months every facility you need will be provided in the palace,” he said, patting the young man approvingly.

The people counted the days, waiting to see the young man’s heroic feat.

He lived a life of pleasure in the palace. He was given foods that strengthened the body, drinks that delighted the heart, and everything he asked for.

The king kept telling his ministers that if the young man succeeded in carrying out the plans he had announced, he would appoint him commander-in-chief of the army.

Three months passed. The day was announced on which the first plan—the plan to walk carrying the mountain on his head—would be fulfilled. The whole country gathered at the foot of the mountain. The king, the great ministers and the palace officials took their respective places.

The young man arrived in a colourful chariot and stepped down. His body shone with new vigour and strength. The effect of three months of palace life was clearly visible in his physique.

Seeing him, the king and the people welcomed him with applause. Drums thundered. Musical instruments sounded. The young man raised both hands. Every sound stopped and silence settled. He walked toward the foot of the mountain. Without blinking, everyone watched the feat that was about to take place.

“I am ready to walk carrying the mountain on my head!” he shouted. A great roar of delight went up.

But what happened? He did not lift the mountain. Again and again he merely repeated, “I am ready to walk carrying the mountain on my head...”

“Why do you keep saying the same thing? Show us by doing it!” the king said in a loud voice.

The young man replied;

“Your Majesty; I said that I would walk carrying the mountain on my head. Do not forget that. All of you join together right now and lift this mountain onto my head. Then you can see whether or not I walk through the whole country carrying it!”
