# It Wouldn't Occur to a Scientist...

<!-- source scan 29; printed page 28 -->

A *mugaththana* scientist kept a beautiful cat. He made a large hole in the door so that it could go in and out. The cat too kept coming and going through it. A few days later the cat had a kitten.

That great scientist made another small hole beside the large opening. His friends asked what it was for. He said it was for the kitten to come and go. Only after they asked, “Can't the kitten also come and go through the large hole made for the cat?” did that scientist think, “Ah! This idea never occurred to me.”
