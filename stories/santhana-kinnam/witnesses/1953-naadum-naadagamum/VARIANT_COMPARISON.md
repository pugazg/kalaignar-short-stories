# Variant comparison — சந்தனக்கிண்ணம் — 1953 witness ↔ 1977 canonical

## Authority and method

The user-supplied **1953 first edition of `நாடும் நாடகமும்`** is an independent comparison witness only. The verified **1977 first-edition anthology** remains canonical.

The complete 1953 story, scans **37–51 / printed 29–43**, was read end-to-end from the controlling scan pixels and compared against the canonical 1977 assembly at `stories/santhana-kinnam/sections/santhana-kinnam.md`.

## Structural finding

**Same complete story / no major scene-level addition or omission.**

The wedding, embedded martial poem, Vijaya's gift, married-life interlude, anti-Hindi agitation, imprisonment, post-prison poverty, pawned bowl, Kamala's death, and retrospective ending all correspond in the same narrative order.

The editions do, however, contain genuine **lexical, syntactic, ideological, punctuation, spacing, and small phrase-level revisions**. The strongest changes occur in the martial poem, the language-agitation victory paragraph, and the final paragraph.

### Important non-confirmation — `தமிழ்த்தாய்கள்`

The 1953 witness reads **`தமிழ்த்தாய்கள்`**, while the exact 1977 controlling scan was previously rechecked at high/native resolution and reads **`தமிழ்த்தாய்களை`**.

Disposition: **true edition difference**. The witness does not reopen or weaken the 1977 source-proven repair.

## Highest-value edition finding — final paragraph

The 1953 ending explicitly says:

`திராவிட நாடு கிடைத்ததும். அந்த விடுதலைப் போரின் வெற்றி விழாவிலே...`

The 1977 canonical edition instead has:

`கிடைத்ததும் மீண்டும் இலட்சியப் போரின் வெற்றி விழாவிலே...`

This is not a transcription error. It is a substantive edition-level rewrite in the retrospective closing paragraph. Both forms are preserved in their respective source layers.
