# Neeyum Kaithi - Naanum Kaithi

<!-- source scan 5; printed page 4 -->

Crescent! Foam of the ocean of the sky! Why do you peer into the prison through the window and laugh at me?

I hear you calling me, ‘Prisoner!’ But what about you? Do you roam about riding a chariot of freedom? Without crossing the line drawn for you, without closing your wakeful eyes, you have only the right to keep moving like a prison guard!

I cannot leap to the beloved who stands yearning outside the prison walls. And what kind of life do you have! You too cannot stretch out your hand and touch the starry ladies who wink beyond your boundary!

I too am a prisoner.

You too are a prisoner.
