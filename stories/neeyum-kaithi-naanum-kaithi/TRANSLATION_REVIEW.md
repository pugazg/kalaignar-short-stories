## Source-sensitive translation choices

- `பிறையே! வானக் கடலையின் நுரையே!` is rendered as **“Crescent! Foam of the ocean of the sky!”**, preserving the direct apostrophe and image.
- `கிழித்த கோட்டைத் தாண்டாமல்` is rendered conservatively as **“without crossing the line drawn for you”** rather than importing a more specific astronomical explanation.
- `உனக்கென்ன வாழ்கிறதாம்!` remains rhetorically unusual in English as **“And what kind of life do you have!”**; the Tamil is not normalized.
- `நானும் கைதி.` / `நீயும் கைதி.` remain two separate closing display paragraphs.
