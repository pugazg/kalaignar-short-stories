## Names, cultural terms and source-sensitive choices

- `அருள்`, `பூபதி`, `பிரபா`, and `கோமதியம்மாள்` are rendered **Arul, Bhoopathi, Prabha**, and **Gomathiyammal**.
- `அத்தான்` is retained as **Aththan** in Prabha’s direct address because it is a kinship-inflected spousal address whose full social relation is not replaced by a generic English endearment.
- the in-story book title `நண்பன்` is rendered **Friend**.
- `மார்கழித் திருநாள்` is rendered **Margazhi festival** without importing outside calendrical explanation.
- `நீத்தார் நினைவு` is rendered conservatively as **memorial rites for the dead**.
- `வைப்பாட்டி` is rendered **kept woman**, preserving the accusation/social register.
- unusual source `வெள்ளாட்டியானேன்` is not silently normalized: the English retains **vellatti** immediately before the source’s clearer `வைப்பாட்டியானேன்` / **kept woman**.
- `காசு` / `காசம்` wordplay is preserved as far as English permits through **money** / **consumption**.
- unusual `நித்தியகண்டம் பூரண ஆயுசாக` is rendered minimally as **“full life, perpetual peril”**, preserving the source’s paradox rather than supplying an external idiomatic explanation.
- source-sensitive `சாறற்ற சக்கை` is rendered **juiceless husk**, retaining the dehumanizing metaphor.
- `வெள்ளிக்கூஜா` is rendered **silver jug**.
- mythological references to Tara/Chandra, Ahalya/Indra, Anasuya/the Trimurti, Sugriva/Rama and others are translated from the canonical Tamil only; no outside mythological correction or commentary is imported.

## Source-sensitive / difficult passages

The translation follows the verified Tamil even where the wording is unusual:

- `மொந்தையில்` is represented through the immediate “new pot / old wine” image without correcting the Tamil;
- the source’s euphemistic description of Prabha’s mother as `ஒருமாதிரி` is retained as “a certain sort”;
- `வழுக்கி விழுந்தவளானாலும்` is rendered as having “slipped and fallen in life,” preserving the metaphor rather than adding a biography;
- `அவர்களைக் காசம் பற்றிக்கொண்டது` is translated by immediate sense as consumption taking hold of Prabha’s mother; the Tamil itself remains unchanged;
- `மலேயாவில் தாங்கள் விலாசத்தையும்` is rendered as Arul’s address in Malaya without correcting the source’s possessive construction;
- no English expectation was used to reopen or modify Tamil.
