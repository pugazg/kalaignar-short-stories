## Difficult / source-sensitive choices

The complete `POSSIBLE_ERRORS_FOR_REVIEW.md` queue was read before translation. No queued Tamil form was silently corrected.

- the protagonist remains **Paruk**, following the repository's source spelling `பருக்`; no outside normalization to another personal-name spelling was introduced.
- `தண்டவாள மணி` is rendered conservatively as the school's **rail-gong**, retaining the physical bell image rather than substituting a generic modern school bell.
- `கார்சேவை` is represented as **karseva** in the English narrative; the Tamil source remains untouched.
- `தம்பித்துரை` is retained as the personal name **Thambithurai**.
- source `இரண்டே தெருக்கள்` remains **only two streets left** before home.
- source `டேப் டான்ஸா?` is rendered by its immediate English sense as **“A tap dance?”**; this is a translation choice, not a Tamil normalization.
- source onomatopoeic `படக் என்று பதில் சொன்னான்` is represented as Paruk **“shot back at once”** rather than inventing an English sound effect.
- the physical 22→23 colloquial join `படிக்காத ராவுத்தருக்கு புள்ளையா / பொறந்து...` is kept as a single continuing statement across the page marker.
- source `ஒரு கரண்டியோ இரண்டு கரண்டியோ` is translated exactly as **one spoonful or two**; no fruit-count interpretation was substituted.
