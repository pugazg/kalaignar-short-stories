## Difficult / source-sensitive choices

- The title `காசா லேசா` is rendered **“Is Cash Easy?”**. `காசு` is represented as “cash” rather than the broader “money” because the story repeatedly turns on bribes, notes, cheques and the final cash-based wordplay.
- Source-layer title/header variants remain untouched in Tamil: opening `காசா லேசா`, running-header `காசா லேசா!`, and scan-40 joined header `காசாலேசா!` are not normalized through the English layer.
- The satirical personal name `லஞ்சய்யா` is transliterated as **Lanjayya** rather than replaced by an explanatory invented English name. The Tamil association with `லஞ்சம்` (bribe) remains implicit, as in the source.
- `திருவுளச் சீட்டு` is rendered contextually as the **drawing of a lot**, preserving the mechanism by which the tied election is decided without importing a modern procedural label.
- `காலணா காசு`, one of the closed historical-glyph corrections, is rendered **“a quarter-anna coin.”** The Tamil source was not altered.
- Source-odd scan-32 `ஒன்றுயின்` is rendered minimally by meaning as the Ganga and Kaveri **“become one.”** This is an English interpretive rendering only; the verified Tamil remains unchanged.
- Source-odd scan-32 `எழெட்டு` is rendered conservatively as **“seven or eight”** stars, without repairing the Tamil source.
- Source-odd scan-33 `கண்ணெல்லாம் புண்ணை` is kept close to the printed wording as **“her eyes all sores.”** No medical interpretation was invented.
- The source’s hospital and police satire, including harsh caste/social insults and dehumanizing rhetoric, is translated as historical narrative language rather than softened or editorially endorsed.
- The scan-39 missing opening quotation before `நான் அடுத்த ஜென்மம்...` is reflected by leaving that English speech without a matching opening quotation; the anomaly is not silently repaired in the source layer.
- The climactic Tamil pun `காசாலேசா! காசாலே; நீ; சா!` cannot be reproduced phoneme-for-phoneme in English. It is rendered **“Cash—easy? By cash; you; die!”**, retaining the semantic double movement from the title phrase to death by cash. The verified Tamil wording remains authoritative.
- The source-odd scan-39 line `மிஸ்டர் லஞ்சய்யா, “செக்! கைப் பார்க்கிறார்; ஐம்பது லட்சம்”.` is rendered conservatively as Lanjayya looking at the cheque in his hand and seeing fifty lakhs. No Tamil correction was made.
