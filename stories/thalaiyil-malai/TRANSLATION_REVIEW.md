## Source-sensitive choices

- `பீநடை` remains untouched in canonical Tamil. The English uses **“a swaggering gait”** from immediate narrative context without asserting a normalized Tamil source form.
- unusual source grammar `அமர்ச்சியப்படுத்தாமல்` remains untouched. English renders its immediate syntactic function conservatively as the king questioning the youth **without having him seated**.
- previously corrected source readings `இயலாதது`, `எடுத்துக் கொள்க!`, and the scan-15 sentence beginning `அடே! அப்படியொரு ஆசையிருந்தால்` are translated from the current verified Tamil and were not reopened.
- the colloquial rebuke `அரண்மனையில் தின்று தொலைத்திருக்கலாமே?` is rendered as **“eaten your fill at the palace”**, preserving the king's scolding tone without changing the Tamil layer.
- `ஆராதனைகள்` is rendered as **adulation** in the youth's explanation of why spectacular promises attract attention.
- `திருக்குறள் புழக்கத்தில் உள்ளதா?` is rendered conservatively as whether the Tirukkural was **in use here**, without external historical gloss.
- `அரசரே அறியார் திருக்குறளை` is rendered from the verified wording as the youth hearing that **even the king did not know the Tirukkural**.
- no outside numbering or conventional English translation of the Tirukkural was imported. The two-line English rendering was made directly from the Tamil block preserved in this story.
