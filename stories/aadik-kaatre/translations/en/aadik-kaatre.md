# O Wind of Aadi!

<!-- source scan 24; printed page 23 -->

O wind of Aadi, why do you shake the trees like this?
Why do you make them dance as a sorcerer makes a spirit dance?
Why do you make them dance as ministers make the people dance? Why do you torment and torture them? What crime did those trees commit? Was it wrong to flourish? Was it wrong to give abundance? Was it wrong to undergo a renaissance? Was it wrong to stand like enchantresses of beauty, wearing fragrant flowers in the coiffures of their branches? What was wrong? Why do you punish them, O wind of Aadi! Tell us the reason! You will not! Like you, the government too gave us no reason! O wind of Aadi! Do you think we will be frightened if you scoop up earth and fling it into our eyes? Are the grains of sand hurled by your hands harsher than the deployment of tear gas?

Dictators too roared hideously like you. They staged frenzies of lawlessness. You and they are of the same breed. O wind of Aadi, howl out that truth!... Once Aadi is over, you will subside. After you subside, you will go to meet the government friend who, like you, put on such a show — your intimate companion! Alas, poor fellow! By then he will have gone into a hole before you.
