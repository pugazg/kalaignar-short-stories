## Title, names and key-term treatment

- `பிரேத விசாரணை` is retained as **Pretha Visaranai** rather than replaced with a single interpretive title.
- `பிச்சுமூர்த்தி`, `கந்தசாமி நாயுடு`, `கருப்பாயி`, `காத்தமுத்து`, `அண்ணாமலை முதலியார்`, `பார்த்தசாரதி அய்யங்கார்`, and `கண்ணாயிரம் பிள்ளை` are rendered **Pichumoorthi, Kandasami Naidu, Karuppayi, Kathamuthu, Annamalai Mudaliar, Parthasarathi Iyengar**, and **Kannayiram Pillai**.
- `அரிஜன` is rendered **Harijan** because that is the historical source term used in the story; it is not silently replaced with a contemporary term.
- source-printed caste-language forms such as `பறைச்சி`, `பற நாயே`, and `பள்ளு பறைகளே` are represented source-close as **paraichi**, **para dog**, and **Pallu-Parai people** within the historical discriminatory speech. Their presence in translation records the source; it is not endorsement.
- `புறம்போக்கு` does not occur as a key issue here; institutional/source terms such as `பண்ணை`, `கும்பாபிஷேகம்`, `மூஷிக விநாயகர்`, `கர்ப்பக் கிரகம்`, and `தேசப் பிரஷ்டம்` are translated or transliterated conservatively.
- `கண்ணப்புக் கண்ணத்தபடி` remains **kannappu-kannathapadi** because the verified phrase is opaque.
