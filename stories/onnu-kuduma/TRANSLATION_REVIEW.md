## Source-sensitive translation choices

- The title **`ஒண்ணு குடுமா?`** is kept as **Onnu Kuduma?** rather than replaced with a single English gloss because the colloquial `ஒண்ணு` / “one” ambiguity drives the mango-versus-kiss joke. Inside the dialogue, `ஒண்ணு கொடுத்திடு` and `ஒண்ணு குடும்மா!` are rendered naturally as **“give him one”**, preserving that ambiguity.
- `புல்கட்டு` is rendered conservatively as **“a bundle of grass”**; the verified Tamil compound itself is not normalized.
- corrected source `உயர் ஜாதிக்காரனுக்குக்` is translated as **“to a high-caste man”** from the current verified Tamil. The earlier rejected reading is not reintroduced.
- `ஆதிதிராவிடப் பெண்` is retained as **“an Adi Dravidar woman”** rather than replaced by an outside modern identity label.
- `அய்யராக` is rendered as **“as an Iyer”** without adding historical or film-context explanation.
- Kalaivaanar and Udumalai Narayana Kavi are left under the source names/titles; no outside biographical identification is inserted.
