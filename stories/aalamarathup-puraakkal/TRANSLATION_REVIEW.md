## Title, names and key-term treatment

- the title `ஆலமரத்துப் புறாக்கள்` is retained as **Aalamarathup Puraakkal**.
- because the story body repeatedly prints `புறு` / `புறுக்கள்` where the title prints `புறாக்கள்`, the English uses **purru** in the allegorical body rather than silently regularizing that edition-level variation.
- compound source forms `சங்கீதப்புறு`, `கவர்ப்புறு`, `கரும்புறு`, and `வெண்புறு` are represented respectively as **sangeetha-purru, kavar-purru, black purru**, and **white purru**.
- `வல்லூறு` is retained as **valluuru** rather than assigned a possibly over-specific English bird identification.
- `கங்காணி` is retained as **kangani**.
- `கொள்வினை கொடுப்பினை`, `விடுதலை விருத்தம்`, and `வெற்றி முரசொலி` are retained source-close as transliterated political/rhetorical labels rather than expanded with outside explanation.
- quoted source forms `“தல”யால்` and `“சக்தி”யைத்` are retained as **“thala”** and **“sakthi”**.
