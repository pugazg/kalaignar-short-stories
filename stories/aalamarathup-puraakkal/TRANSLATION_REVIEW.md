## 2026 synchronization

The dual-gate re-audit changed canonical Tamil:

- one Gate-A wording repair on scan 151: first `ஓர் இளத்த புறா` → `ஓர் இளம் புறா`;

The earlier English had deliberately transliterated the legacy misread as **`purru`**. That rationale is no longer valid. The English is therefore synchronized by changing **83 affected `purru` tokens to `puraa`** while preserving the existing sentence structure, source-page markers and allegorical labels.

Examples:

- `sangeetha-purru` → `sangeetha-puraa`
- `black purru` → `black puraa`
- `white purru` → `white puraa`
- `kavar-purru` → `kavar-puraa`

Ordinary plural narration that already used **pigeons** remains unchanged.
