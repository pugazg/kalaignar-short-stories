When a ninety-year-old man was digging a pit in a garden and planting a mango seed, some people are said to have asked him, “Old man, how old are you? You may have another two years, or five years, to live. Yet you are planting this mango seed. When will it become a sapling, grow into a tree, put out branches, sprout leaves, flower, bear unripe fruit, ripen, and finally give you a fruit to eat?” The old man is said to have replied, “If my grandfather had thought that way, I could not have eaten the fruit of that mango tree. So I am doing this for my grandchildren in the future, for my descendants.”

In the same way, when will all the national languages in India become official languages
