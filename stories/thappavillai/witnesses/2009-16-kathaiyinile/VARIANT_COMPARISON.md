# Variant comparison — தப்பவில்லை — 1977 canonical ↔ 2009 witness

## Coverage

2009 scans **137–149 / printed 132–144** directly inspected from the supplied scan copy; scan **150** checked as the `ஏழை` opening boundary.

## Finding

The 2009 edition represents the same story rather than an expanded recension. It retains the prison frame, the retrospective account of the conviction, and the final ironic `நிரபராதிகள் தண்டிக்கப்படமாட்டார்கள்!` line. Scan 149 ends with six ornaments.

Differences observed are predominantly later editorial regularization: punctuation, dialogue marks, internal spacing, sandhi, and selected colloquial spellings. The 2009 layout spreads the text over **13 scans**, compared with **10 canonical 1977 scans**, but no 2009-only narrative block was identified.

The canonical recheck queue therefore remains a controlling-source question, not a later-edition correction map. Canonical 1977 Tamil and English changed: **No**.
