## Authority and method

The attached 1950 `வாழமுடியாதவர்கள்` collection is an earlier comparison witness only. The verified 1977 first-edition anthology remains canonical.

The complete 1950 story, scans **25–29 / printed 24–28**, was read from direct scan pixels and compared by text anchors against the verified canonical assembly. No OCR, web copy or substitute edition controls this comparison. Routine line wrapping is not treated as a textual variant.

## Orthography / spacing / punctuation classes

Recurrent non-structural edition differences include:

- joined vs split auxiliaries and compounds;
- 1950 use of `பாபம்` / `பாபந்தான்` where 1977 sometimes uses `பாவம்`;
- comma, dash, question-mark and ellipsis density;
- dialogue-quote spacing;
- `ஒருநாள்` ↔ `ஒரு நாள்`;
- `பிரமாவை யல்லவா` / `பிரமாவையல்லவா` type spacing differences.

These are retained as witness evidence rather than normalized.
