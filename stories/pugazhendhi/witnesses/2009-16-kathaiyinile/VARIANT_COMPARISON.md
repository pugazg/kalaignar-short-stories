# Variant comparison — புகழேந்தி — 1977 canonical ↔ 2009 witness

## Comparison policy

The 1977 first-edition anthology remains the controlling source for the canonical workspace. The 2009 fourth edition is an **independent additional witness**. A later reading is therefore evidence of edition history, not automatic permission to rewrite the 1977 transcription.

All 2009 scans **82–89 / printed 77–84** were directly visually inspected. Scan **90** was inspected only to confirm the `நளாயினி` boundary.

## Overall finding

The 2009 edition preserves the same story sequence, same final situation and same `மேதை வாழ்க!` close, but it contains systematic editorial modernization/regularization and several lexical readings that are valuable for rechecking suspicious 1977 spans. No added epilogue or comparable narrative expansion was identified.

## Strong canonical recheck candidates

The 2009 witness materially strengthens the case for reopening these exact 1977 spans when the controlling PDF is available:

1. **`புகழ்தரும் தீவலி` ↔ `புகழ்தரும் தலைவலி`** — the later reading is directly legible and contextually transparent; 1977 must nevertheless be checked at scan 11 / printed page 2 before correction.
2. **`வயித்துக்கிடக்கிறது` ↔ `லயித்துக் கிடக்கிறது`** — the later edition supplies a coherent verb phrase; the canonical form is already in the human recheck queue.
3. **`காதற் கண்கள்` ↔ `காதற் கணைகள்`** — the later source clearly prints `கணைகள்`, fitting the arrow metaphor; this is a high-value glyph/source recheck for 1977 scan 14 / printed page 5.
4. **`கால்ப் பணிவிடைகள்` ↔ `காலைப் பணிவிடைகள்`** — later reading clearly resolves the compound differently; re-open 1977 scan 15 / printed page 6 before any source-layer change.
5. **`அவனோர் பிடேல்டோ!` ↔ `அவனொரு பிடேல்டோ!`** — later edition regularizes the construction; useful but less decisive because editorial rewriting is plausible.
6. **`மணக்கும் அவன் நெஞ்சம்.` ↔ `மனக்கும் அவன் நெஞ்சம்`** — a genuine divergent reading; neither should be selected for 1977 without its scan.

## Structural / presentation differences

- **Opening:** the 2009 edition uses a boxed `புகழேந்தி` title treatment on scan 82. The canonical 1977 source has a centered heading with an ornamented horizontal rule. This is edition-specific visual presentation.
- **Pagination:** 1977 occupies six printed pages; 2009 occupies eight. The extra pages arise from later typesetting/layout, not from a newly added ending or epilogue.
- **Display refrain:** both editions isolate the escalating `புகழ்! புகழ்!! புகழ்!!!` line, corroborating its special display status.
- **Ending:** both editions close after the students/teachers' `மேதை வாழ்க!` cry. The 2009 source then prints **six circular floral ornaments**. The represented 1977 source records a different horizontal-rule/three-central-mark closing ornament.
- **Boundary:** 2009 scan 90 visibly opens `நளாயினி`; no `நளாயினி` text belongs to this comparison.

## Canonical disposition

The current 1977 page layer, assembled Tamil, English translation, page statuses and visual-fidelity PASS remain unchanged. The additional witness only augments the provenance/recheck record until direct 1977 controlling-source evidence is available.
