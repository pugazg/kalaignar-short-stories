# The King and the Sparrow!

> **English translation of the verified Tamil archival reading.** Controlling source: 1987 collection `கலைஞர் சொன்ன குட்டிக் கதைகள்`, scan **5 / printed page 4 → upper scan 6 / printed page 5**. The verified Tamil remains authoritative. Physical source-page boundaries are retained for traceability.

<!-- anthology scan: 5; printed page: 4 -->

A king was walking along the verandah of a palace. Just then, a tiny sparrow, having stolen a coin from somewhere, came and perched on that verandah, held the coin under its foot, looked at the king, and mockingly asked three times, “I’ve got a coin. Who wants it?”

If the king had simply kept going on his way, there would have been no trouble. Instead, he made much of what the sparrow had said and, looking at it, said, “Why do you keep saying that again and again for no reason? Give that coin to me!”

The sparrow immediately gave the coin to the king and kept saying, “The king took alms from me.”

At once the king became angry.... “Tch! Did I take alms from you? Here, take your coin,” he said, and threw it back! Even then the sparrow would not keep quiet. “The king got frightened of me and gave the coin back,” it said.

If the king had simply gone on his own way, thinking, “Let us go our way,” there would have been no need for him to hear such words from a mere sparrow, would there! Why did he turn back! Why did he ask, “Give it to me”! Why did he go and let the sparrow pin the label of coward on him!

Bearing in mind the way AIADMK Propaganda Secretary Mrs Jayalalithaa has been speaking disparagingly of Leader Kalaignar and the frontline leaders, the Leader told this story precisely to point out that such remarks should not be taken seriously.
