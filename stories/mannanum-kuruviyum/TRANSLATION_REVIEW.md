## Translation choices

- `மன்னனும் குருவியும்!` is rendered **The King and the Sparrow!**; `குருவி` is translated as “sparrow” rather than transliterated.
- colloquial `என்கிட்ட ஒருகாசு இருக்கு. யாருக்கு வேணும்?` is rendered naturally as **“I’ve got a coin. Who wants it?”** while retaining the taunting tone.
- `என்கிட்ட பிச்சை வாங்கின ராஜா` is rendered **“The king took alms from me.”** This keeps the source accusation without expanding it into a stronger claim than the Tamil supports.
- `பயந்தாங்கொள்ளி பட்டத்தை ... வாங்கிக் கட்டிக் கொள்வானேன்` is rendered as letting the sparrow **“pin the label of coward on him”**, preserving the rhetorical sense rather than literally translating the idiom word by word.
- the final source paragraph is treated as part of the story record and translated in the same position. `அ.தி.மு.க.` is rendered **AIADMK**, `கொள்கை பரப்புச் செயலாளர்` as **Propaganda Secretary**, `தலைவர் கலைஞர்` as **Leader Kalaignar**, and `முன்னணித் தலைவர்கள்` as **frontline leaders**. No outside political explanation was inserted.
