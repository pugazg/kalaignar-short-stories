You know the story of Kumanan.

Kumanan had a younger brother, said to be called Amanan. In order to seize his elder brother's

<!-- source scan 63; printed page 61 -->

kingdom, he devised a plot and sent his elder brother Kumanan into the forest. His treachery did not stop there: thinking that Kumanan might raise an army, return and recover the country, he even planned to have him killed. At that time a great poet, suffering in poverty, came to Kumanan seeking a gift.

But Amanan was in the court where Kumanan had once been. “I have come seeking a gift, seeking wealth,” said the poet. Amanan's proclamation—that ten thousand pieces of gold would be awarded to whoever brought the head of his elder brother who was in the forest—was told to the poet. “Is that so? Is there a price on Kumanan's head?” he asked, and went into the forest to see Kumanan. Kumanan wandered there like an ascetic who had renounced his country. Seeing the poet, he welcomed him warmly: “Come, poet.” The poet told him of his poverty. Kumanan then said, “There is a way to end your poverty.”

“That day had not come, O poet of rare verse;
This day you came in distress and reached me;
Take my head and give it into my younger brother's hand;
Receive its price and cure your sickness of poverty.”

“If you take my head and give it into my younger brother's hands, he will give you a reward of ten thousand pieces of gold. Take it and rid yourself of your poverty,” he said. The poet was shaken by those words. Leaving Kumanan, on his way back he fashioned a likeness of Kumanan's head from a banana plant, made it appear as though blood were flowing from it, and brought it to Amanan's court.

Amanan was seated there. The poet threw down the head of Kumanan made from the banana stem and said, “Look, I have brought your elder brother Kumanan's head.” At the sight of the blood-dripping head, Amanan's body trembled—as in the saying that even if one does not dance, one's own flesh will dance. His heart churned. “Oh, elder brother!” he cried. “Have you killed my brother?” he wept.
