## Source-sensitive choices

- The source opening has a closing quotation mark after `கொண்டு வருகிறேன்”` without a corresponding opening quotation mark before `இருபதாயிரம்`. The English preserves that source-close asymmetry instead of silently inserting an opening quotation mark.
- `வராகன்` is retained conservatively as **varagan** rather than replaced by an outside historical currency identification.
- `அற்புதமானப் பட்டு` remains untouched in Tamil; the immediate sense is rendered as **“wonderful silk”** without asserting a Tamil normalization.
- `கற்புடைய மனைவியைப் பெற்ற கணவர்களின் கண்களுக்கு மட்டுமே தெரியும்` is rendered directly as the silk being visible only to husbands who have chaste wives; no outside moral or cultural gloss is added.
- The final phrase `வெறுங்கையால் முழம் போடுவதற்கு இப்படியொரு பழங்கதை உண்டு` is rendered conservatively as an old tale behind measuring by cubits with an empty hand, preserving the explanatory close rather than modernizing it into an idiom note.
