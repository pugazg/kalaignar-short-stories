Accordingly, the gold was given. After a long time, Tenali Raman came to the royal court. The king eagerly asked, “Where is the silk from Indra’s world?” “Here it is, Your Majesty,” he said, and, as though spreading out and measuring a silk cloth, he measured with empty hands, saying, “One cubit, two cubits,” again and again. The king was stunned. The courtiers were stunned. “This silk is visible only to the eyes of husbands who have chaste wives. Your Majesty, can you see it?” Raman asked. Poor king! What could he say? Before the king could respond, all the courtiers said in one voice, “We can see it, we can see it—wonderful silk.” The king too said, “Yes, I can see it clearly.” There is an old tale like this behind measuring by cubits with an empty hand.
