# A Knot in the Neck... and a Story Behind It

<!-- source scan 27; printed page 26 -->

...Touch your neck once and see. There is a knot in this neck. A bony knot. The information — the fact — that scientific investigation gives us about this bony knot is that it provides the power that helps us bend and straighten the neck and move it this way and that....

Even this bony knot has a Puranic story behind it!...

Once upon a time the devas and the asuras churned the sacred Milk Ocean. The heroic (!) devas held the tail end of the serpent Vasuki, while the cowardly (!) asuras held the serpent's head, and they churned the Milk Ocean using Mount Meru as the churning rod.

On one side, amrita appeared. On another side, Alakala poison appeared.

The devas took the amrita — selfless people!

“Is there no amrita for us?” asked the asuras. “No,” said the devas.

<!-- source scan 28; printed page 27 -->

The Alakala poison surged up. When it surged, everyone — including the devas — realized that it endangered the whole world and ran to Paramasivan, crying, “Protect us! Refuge! Refuge!”

At once Paramasivan, to save all the people, took the surging Alakala poison and put it into his mouth.

Parvati, who was beside him, feared that it would be dangerous if the poison entered Siva's stomach and grasped his neck. Thus the poison did not enter his stomach and stopped at his neck.

The poison that stopped in Siva's neck became a lump. From then on, every man in the world had a lump in his neck...
