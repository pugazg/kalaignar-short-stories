## 2009 structural evidence

The 2009 fourth edition is **not a simple reprint**. It contains internal five-ornament separators and, most importantly, a substantial new epilogue on scans **80–81 / printed 75–76** after the illness/doctor sequence. The represented 1977 canonical edition closes without that epilogue.

This structural difference is documented at:

- `witnesses/2009-16-kathaiyinile/README.md`
- `witnesses/2009-16-kathaiyinile/VARIANT_COMPARISON.md`

The existence of a later epilogue is an edition fact, not a missing-text error in the 1977 transcription unless another source establishes otherwise.
