## Comparison result

**COMPLETE — all scans 64–81 were visually reviewed against the existing 1977 canonical reading layer; scan 82 was checked as the ending boundary.**

Result: the 2009 text is **not a simple reprint**. It contains:

1. widespread punctuation, spacing, sandhi and dialogue-form changes;
2. a number of lexical/wording substitutions;
3. internal five-ornament section breaks not represented in the 1977 canonical edition;
4. a **substantial added epilogue on scans 80–81 / printed pages 75–76**, after a structural break, extending the story three years beyond the illness/doctor scene where the 1977 canonical edition closes.

See [`VARIANT_COMPARISON.md`](VARIANT_COMPARISON.md) for the explicit comparison and the source-faithful 2009-only epilogue.
