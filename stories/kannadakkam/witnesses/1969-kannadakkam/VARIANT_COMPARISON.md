# Variant comparison — கண்ணடக்கம் — 1969 witness ↔ 1977 canonical

## Authority and method

The attached **1969 second edition** is an independent comparison witness only. The verified **1977 first-edition anthology** remains canonical.

The complete 1969 story, scans **4–10 / printed 3–9**, was read end-to-end against the canonical 1977 assembly and page records. Historical Tamil glyph identity was interpreted as character identity rather than modern visual resemblance. Routine line-wrap differences are not treated as textual variants.

No OCR, external website, substitute edition, or silent normalization controls this comparison.

## Structural finding

**Equivalent narrative / no structural expansion.**

The opening situation, Kali dialogue, `கண்ணடக்கம்` explanation, removal of the silver eye-covering, public-health intervention and final eye-hospital awakening all correspond. No scene or prose block is missing from either narrative sequence at comparison level.

1969 has a visible typographic scene-break before the hospital frame; this is layout evidence rather than an added narrative event.

## Historical-glyph caution

The 1969 typography uses period glyphs that can resemble modern characters. Apparent forms were not modernized by shape alone. In particular, the historical reading corresponding to canonical `நானா?` was not treated as a `நானு` lexical variant merely from visual resemblance.

The 1969 `வாளை` reading is supported by its historical `ளை` character identity and by the independent 2009 witness; it is therefore recorded explicitly.
