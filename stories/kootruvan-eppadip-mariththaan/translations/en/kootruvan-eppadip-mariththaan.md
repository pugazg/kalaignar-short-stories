# How Did Kootruvan Take Him?

<!-- source scan 35; printed page 34 -->

The poet Nappasalaiyar was a close friend of the great king Killi Valavan...

Killi Valavan dies. The poet asks how he could possibly have died.

Neither by opposing him, nor by befriending him, nor by fighting him could Yama have taken Killi Valavan’s life. Then how could he have taken it?

Like a singer,  
with hands joined in worship and praise,  
he must have begged  
for it.

Yama must have disguised himself as a poet, gone to Killi Valavan and asked him, “Give me your life.” Taking Kootruvan for a poet, Killi Valavan must have given him his life...
