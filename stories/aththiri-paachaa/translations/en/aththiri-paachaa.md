# Aththiri Paachaa

A boy had gone to his grandmother’s house in a neighbouring village. There, his grandmother made him kozhukattai. After tasting and eating it, the grandson eagerly asked, “What is this called, Grandma?” “This is kozhukattai,” said his grandmother. “When I get back to my village, I’m going to tell Amma and ask her to make this at our house too,” said the grandson. Because he was often forgetful, he kept repeating “kozhukattai, kozhukattai” like a mantra as he made his way back home.

“Kozhukattai! Kozhukattai!” he kept muttering as he walked. On the way, a small channel appeared before him, and he cleared it in a single leap. As he jumped, he shouted “Aththirip Paachaa!” The moment he did so, he forgot kozhukattai. Repeating only “Aththirip Paachaa, Aththiri Paachaa,” he reached home and told his mother, “Amma, make me Aththiri Paachaa,” and began to throw a
