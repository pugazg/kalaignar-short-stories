## Source-sensitive choices

- The distinct source forms `காப்புமுற்று`, first `காப்புமுற்றிருக்கின்றது`, later `காப்புமுற்றிருக்கிறது`, and `காப்பு மடைவதாவது?` remain distinct and unchanged in Tamil. Their immediate story sense is rendered conservatively with **became pregnant / is pregnant / become pregnant**; the English does not claim a Tamil normalization.
- `(சிரிப்பு)` and `(பலத்த சிரிப்பு)` are retained as `(Laughter)` and `(Loud laughter)`.
- The opening ironic contrast between the two neighbours is preserved without adding an explicit insult that the source leaves unfinished.
- The jar/baby-jar conceit and final profit-versus-loss observation remain in source order; no outside folklore attribution is inserted.
