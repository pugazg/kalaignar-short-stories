## Authority and method

The attached 1950 collection is an earlier comparison witness only. The verified 1977 first-edition anthology remains canonical.

The 1950 physical span, scans 6–15 / printed 5–14, was inspected from direct scan pixels and compared by text anchors to the verified 1977 canonical assembly. No OCR, web copy or later-edition text was used to manufacture the 1950 witness. Routine line wrapping is not treated as a textual variant.

Where paper is physically absent, the witness is recorded as lost rather than reconstructed.

## Orthography / spacing / punctuation classes

The comparison also shows recurrent edition styling differences that do not change narrative structure, including:

- joined / split compounds such as கதைதான் ↔ கதை தான், குருவிக் கூடு ↔ குருவிக்கூடு, படுக்கை யறை ↔ படுக்கையறை and முணுமுணுத்தபடி ↔ முணு முணுத்தபடி;
- 1950 full-stop / comma / question-mark choices differing from the 1977 assembly;
- 1950 colloquial final -ல்ல forms where the 1977 edition often has -ல்லே;
- spacing around ஏற்றிக் கொண்டு / ஏற்றிக்கொண்டு, செய்து கொண்டு / செய்துகொண்டு and similar auxiliaries;
- source-specific dialogue-quote and dash conventions.

These are preserved as edition-level evidence rather than normalized.
