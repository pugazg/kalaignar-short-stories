- `ஜெகவீரசிம்மன்`, `சத்தியவதி`, `அமிர்தராணி`, `அர்தோல்`, `வீரசிம்மன்` are rendered **Jegaveerasimman, Sathyavathi, Amirtharani, Arthol, Veerasimman**.
- `ராஜபுத்ர`, `பாதுஷா`, `வைகுந்த நாதன்`, `ராமபிரான்`, `பாஷாணம்`, and `ஆலஹாலம்` are handled conservatively without importing outside historical or religious explanation.
- `பாஷாணம்` is retained as **pashaanam** in the poisoned-milk scene rather than assigned a more specific substance unsupported by the source.
- `காஞ்சரங் கனியே` is translated minimally as **kanjarang fruit**, preserving the unusual source metaphor rather than silently replacing it.
- `பேசுகிறாய்க்கும்?`, `என் அண்ணை`, repeated `அண்ண`, `தகாப் போக்குடையோன்`, and the source-joined `அவதூறிலிருந்துகாப்பாற்றுகிறாய்` are rendered from immediate sentence meaning while the Tamil remains unchanged.
- the final `ஆலஹாலம்` is transliterated **Aalahalam**, preserving the source term and its contrast with God.

- the mother’s suspicion is established before Jegaveera leaves, so his later reading of the letter remains psychologically continuous;
