## Notable source-sensitive contrasts

### Printed couplet

The 1950 witness prints:

`குழலினிது யாழ் இனிது...`

The 1982 source-close canonical preserves:

`குழலினது யாழ் இனிது...`

The 1982 workspace already explicitly treats its printed குறள் wording as source-faithful. The 1950 reading is therefore recorded as textual history, not used to silently correct 1982.

### `தலையில் பாரத்தைப்` vs `தீயில் பார்த்ததைப்`

In the Prema / fate passage, the 1950 witness has the coherent phrase:

`ஆண்டவன் தலையில் பாரத்தைப் போட்டவில்லை`

The 1982 canonical has the unusual source-close:

`ஆண்டவன் தீயில் பார்த்ததைப் போட்டவில்லை`

Because the complete 1982 story passed a final direct source/visual audit, the 1950 wording is recorded as an edition variant rather than used to rewrite the canonical.

### `மளிகைக் கடையில்` vs `மனிதைக் கடையில்`

The 1950 social-double-standard analogy begins with `மளிகைக் கடையில்`, while the 1982 source-close canonical has `மனிதைக் கடையில்`.

Again, the earlier reading is semantically ordinary, but source authority is edition-specific. No canonical repair is made from the witness alone.
