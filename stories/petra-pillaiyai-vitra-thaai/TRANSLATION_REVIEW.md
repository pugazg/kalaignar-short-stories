## Difficult / source-sensitive choices

- Title `பெற்ற பிள்ளையை விற்ற தாய்` is rendered **“The Mother Who Sold the Child She Bore”**, preserving the force of `பெற்ற` rather than reducing the title simply to “her child.”
- The scan-8 quoted குறள் is translated from the **actual verified printed wording** in this source. No canonical modern Tamil wording or published English Tirukkural translation was substituted.
- `அவிர்ப்பாகம்` is retained as **avirppagam** on first use and glossed as the sacrificial/ritual portion; later uses retain the source’s satirical medicinal meaning.
- Caste, ritual and social terms such as **Zamindar, Vaidika, Iyengar, putrakameshti, pitambara, panchamirtham, tali, Iyarpagai Nayanar** are retained or lightly glossed where needed rather than culturally flattened.
- Source-odd `இலவுக்காத்தக் கிளி` is rendered conservatively as **“the parrot waiting for the ilavu fruit”**; the Tamil was not normalized.
- Source-odd `முருங்கை மிலார்` is deliberately retained as **“moringa milar”** inside the dialogue rather than silently replacing the Tamil source with a guessed idiom.
- The unusual scan-22 image `மிலேச்சியிலிருந்து மஹாராணக்...` is kept close to its printed terms as **“from a mleccha straight into a Maharana”**; no attempt was made to repair the Tamil source.
- The opaque scan-17→18 clause ending `...ஆண்டவன் தீயில் பார்த்ததைப் போட்டவில்லை` is translated contextually and conservatively as Prema ceasing to lay everything at God’s door. This is an English interpretive choice only; the verified Tamil remains untouched.
- The source’s social vocabulary around sexual conduct, caste, legitimacy and prostitution is translated as historical narrative rhetoric. It is not softened, modernized or endorsed editorially.
