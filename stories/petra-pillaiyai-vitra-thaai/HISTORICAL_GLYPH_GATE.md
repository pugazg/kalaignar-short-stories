- scan 14 `கர்ப்பக்கிரகத்தில்`;
- scan 21 `இலவுக்காத்தக் கிளி`;
- scan 22 the unusual `மிலேச்சியிலிருந்து மஹாராணக் குதித்து விட்டீரே!` comparison phrase;
- scan 24 `தெரியாத்தனம்`;
- scan 26 `முருங்கை மிலார்` and colloquial `இல்ல`.

These survivals are important evidence that the gate decoded glyph identity without rewriting source vocabulary or grammar.

## Gate method completed

For every scan **7–28**:

- the complete page was inspected at native/high resolution;
- all 13 known reform-sensitive families were checked;
- suspicious clusters were compared against full words/phrases and same-edition forms where useful;
- historical character identity was encoded in normal modern Unicode;
- source spelling, grammar, sandhi, vocabulary, spacing and punctuation were otherwise preserved;
- no global replacement was used;
- no correction was made from lexical expectation alone.
