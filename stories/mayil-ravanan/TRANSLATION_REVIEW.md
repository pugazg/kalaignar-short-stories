## Source-sensitive choices

- The corrected verified form `ஊடுதல் செயலாளராக` is unusual and is **not** silently normalized to the earlier `ஊடுருவல்`. Because a confident semantic replacement would exceed the source, the English conservatively renders it as **“ooduthal secretary”** while translating the surrounding `கூடுதல் செயலாளர்` as **additional secretary**.
- `ஆணையிடப் பட்டிருக்கின்ற`, `முப்பத்து முக்கோடி`, `மும்மூர்த்திகளாலும்`, `பளிங்குமண்டபம்`, `உசேன்`, and joined `மயில்ராவணனாகிய` remain untouched in Tamil; English follows the immediate narrative sense without claiming source correction.
- Both `(பலத்த கைதட்டல்)` cues are retained as `(Loud applause)`.
- The puranic nested sequence—underworld, crystal pavilion, stream, lotus stalk, cave, golden structure, casket, beetle—is preserved in source order.
- The political comparison between Usen, Mayil Ravanan and the movement is translated as source content without outside political identification or advocacy.
