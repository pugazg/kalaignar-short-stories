Treasurer Veerasamy mentioned here that we appointed Usen as an additional organisational secretary of the Kazhagam. It is because he will not forever be an “ooduthal secretary” that we have appointed him to the responsibility of additional secretary. At times when this movement faces so many trials and sufferings, many people in political parties wonder how it is held together and protected, how it confronts all those trials and rises again with renewed vigour. In particular, there is amazement at how I, who have been charged with leading this movement, am able, even after such great trials, sufferings, whirlwinds, storms and earthquakes, to stand

<!-- source scan 31; printed page 29 -->

upright. Many party people, and many among the general public too, have that amazement.

If I have to tell all of them one thing: in the story of Mayil Ravanan, even the thirty-three crore devas could not capture Mayil Ravanan. Nor could the Trimurti whom they appealed to and brought there. Then, when they wondered what way there was to bring about Mayil Ravanan’s fall, they secretly discovered it.

In the underworld there is a crystal pavilion. There is a stream there. If one jumps into that stream and dives beneath the water, there is a lotus stalk. If one goes inside through that lotus stalk, there is a large cave. If one enters that cave, there is a great golden structure. Inside that structure is a casket. Inside the casket is a beetle. The purana says that if that beetle is taken out and crushed, Mayil Ravanan can be brought down. That beetle is Usen. (Loud applause)

Because people like Usen are such beetles within this movement, no one has been able to bring down me, the Mayil Ravanan, or this movement that I lead. (Loud applause)
