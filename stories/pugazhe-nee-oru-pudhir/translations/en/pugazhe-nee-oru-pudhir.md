# Fame, You Are a Riddle

<!-- source scan 31; printed page 30 -->

Fame! You are a block of ice. Even if I keep you in my hand and grip you tightly, you melt into water and disappear.

Fame! You are a jar of liquor. The flies that fall into you never rise again.

<!-- source scan 32; printed page 31 -->

Fame! You are a shadow. You keep following those who do not care about you.

Fame! You are a riddle! I cannot explain you.
