# The Ascetic and the Flower Seller

> **English translation of the verified Tamil archival reading.** Controlling source: 1987 collection `கலைஞர் சொன்ன குட்டிக் கதைகள்`, lower scan **24 / printed page 23 → upper scan 25 / printed page 24**. The verified Tamil remains authoritative.

<!-- anthology scan: 24; printed page: 23; source span: lower -->

Whenever an ascetic passed by the temple, a woman selling flowers there would look at the

<!-- anthology scan: 25; printed page: 24; source span: upper -->

ascetic and keep asking, “Well, ascetic? When is the wedding?” The first day she asked him like that, the ascetic glared and went away. The second day, when the flower seller asked the same question, the ascetic looked at her a little more kindly and went away. The third day she asked, the ascetic gave her a sidelong glance and went away.

On the fourth day, when she asked, the ascetic said, “I am ready for the wedding right now! Come! Here is the thaali!”

In the same way, they kept asking, “Who is the Chief Minister? Who is the Chief Minister?” That created a situation in which I had to answer that I was the Chief Minister. Therefore it was M.G.R. who created that necessity.

(This story was told during the 1980 Legislative Assembly election.)
