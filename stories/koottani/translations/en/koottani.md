are said to have tied their legs together and gone in procession all over the town.

As they were going along like this, they came upon a small pond. Once the frog saw that pond, could it forget its nature? Suddenly the frog jumped into the pond. The mouse did not know how to swim. The mouse floated in the water. How long could it stay afloat? The mouse sank and died. After it died, a hawk circling in the sky at that moment spotted the dead mouse floating in the pond. Ah! It had found a good meal. The hawk swooped down in a single rush and carried the mouse away. When it carried off the mouse, the frog tied to it went along as well. “We thought we had one prey. What we got was two prey,” and the hawk ate them both.
