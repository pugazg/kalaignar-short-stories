# Riddle

“Mother Poovazhagi! Among grains you easily identify the chaff and throw it aside. But there is chaff among people too. How can one identify them?”

Not only Poovazhagi—all the women working with her stood bewildered. At that moment Kaarmegam’s son-in-law Kannaayiram came there. When they saw him, they moved aside.

“What is this? What are the little sparrows saying? If my father-in-law gets an opportunity, then an opportunity it is! Young doves are always dancing—ah! This threshing floor is nothing but one great revel!”

Kannaayiram said this, and Kaarmegam grew angry.

<!-- source scan 42; printed page 41 -->

“Hey, don’t talk like that. Every one of them is like a daughter born to me!”

Hearing this, Kannaayiram laughed aloud.

“What is this, father-in-law? You speak without knowing the world! Whichever one of these women you touch would wilt! What, little ones; am I not right?”

Until then Poovazhagi had stood patiently among the group of women. Now she burst out like a storm and stood before Kaarmegam.

“Sir! You asked how to identify chaff among people. There is no need to go searching for it. Here—this very figure is the chaff among people!” she shouted, anger surging in her voice.
