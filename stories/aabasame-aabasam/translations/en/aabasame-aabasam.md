# Obscenity Is Obscene!

<!-- source scan 23; printed page 22 -->

That Ayyanar was born to Ari and Aran — that is, to a man and a man — is said not to be obscene. Calling it obscene is said to be obscene!

The story that Sivanar raped the wives of the rishis of Darukavana is said not to be obscene. Calling it obscene is said to be obscene!

<!-- source scan 24; printed page 23 -->

The Trimurtis demanding of Anu Suya, “Come completely naked and serve us food,” is said not to be obscene. Declaring that these foolish tales should not be believed is said to be obscene!
