# Manaivi Sonna Vilakkam

This afternoon at 3 o'clock I listened to a *vaaliba naadagam*. In that drama a woman named Chandana is put up for sale in a market, with a price called out. A very beautiful woman, an extraordinary beauty... A great wealthy gentleman buys the woman and takes her to his town. On reaching the town, he calls the one who is in the pleasure garden—

<!-- source scan 14; printed page 13 -->

his wife—and says, “My dearest wife! I have bought and brought home a beautiful slave.” Delighted that she has got a servant, the wife says, ‘Remove her veil; let me see.’

When the veil is removed accordingly, she sees that the woman is extraordinarily beautiful. The gentleman tells his wife, “Look after her; I shall attend to some business matters and return,” and leaves for another town. After he goes, his wife thinks: ‘He has not bought this beauty for work. He has brought her to satisfy his own desire. So, before he returns, I must make this beauty ugly.’ She has a barber summoned, tells him to cut the woman's hair crookedly and unevenly, and has crooked lines burned and scored across her face, making her grotesque!

As her reason, she says, ‘My husband will fall in love with her. That is why I am doing this!’
