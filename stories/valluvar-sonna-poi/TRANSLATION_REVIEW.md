## Source-sensitive translation choices

- The title remains **Valluvar Sonna Poi**, a transliteration, rather than replacing the source title with an interpretive English title.
- `குறள்` is rendered conservatively as ***kural***. No outside identification or explanatory attribution is inserted into the story body.
- `எங்கேல்லாமோ` is rendered as **“all over the place”**, preserving the indefinite roaming sense without treating the unusual printed form as a Tamil error.
- Joined `தன்வீட்டிற்குள்ளே` is translated for meaning as **“into his own house”**; the verified Tamil orthography is left unchanged.
- Source-separated `அந்த மான் குட்டியை` is represented semantically as **“that fawn”**; no Tamil normalization is made.
- Quoted `‘வரவில்லை’` is represented with single quotation marks as **‘It did not come.’**
- The source contrast between `உண்மை` and `வாய்மை` is preserved by rendering the former as **truth** while retaining ***vaaymai*** with the first-use gloss **“truthful speech”**. This avoids flattening two distinct source terms into the same English word.
- The source's double exclamation in `வாய்மையா, அல்லவா!!` is retained as **“or not!!”**.
- The closing `பொய்மையும் அந்த வாய்மையிடத்தில் வைக்கப்படும்` is rendered conservatively as **“even the lie I told will be placed in the place of that vaaymai.”** No conventional Tirukkural wording or outside paraphrase is substituted.
