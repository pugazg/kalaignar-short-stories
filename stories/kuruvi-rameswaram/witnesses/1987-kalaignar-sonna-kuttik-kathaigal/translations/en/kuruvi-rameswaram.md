# Kuruvi Rameswaram

> **English translation of the verified 1987 witness reading.** This translation belongs only to the 1987 `கலைஞர் சொன்ன குட்டிக் கதைகள்` witness. The controlling 2004 Tamil and English at `stories/kuruvi-rameswaram/` remain unchanged. Source-specific 1987 forms are translated without importing the 2004 wording.

<!-- anthology scan: 23; printed page: 22; lower shared span -->

Said while speaking at the Erode Chikkayya Naicker College Student Council function :-

A consolation for all the students! When you garlanded me and when you draped handloom cloths on me, you said, “We have organized this student gathering like a World Tamil Nadu.”

There is an oral-tradition tale in the Ramayana. It is said that while Rama was wandering in search of Sita, he came near our Tiruvarur.

When he came there, a sparrow bowed to him and asked for a boon. When Rama asked what boon it wanted, the sparrow said that it wanted to go to Rameswaram.

The sparrow's wings were very small. Rameswaram was a faraway town. “You cannot fly such a long distance. Therefore, little sparrow, for you—”

<!-- anthology scan: 24; printed page: 23; upper shared span -->

“—I will create a Rameswaram right here,” Rama is said to have told it. With the tip of his arrow he drew a line there and created a small pond, saying that if the sparrow bathed in it, it would gain merit.

The story says that the sparrow listened to Rama, bathed in the pond drawn out by his arrow, and gained merit. Even now that place is called Kuruvi Rameswaram.

It is the same with me, unable to go to Rameswaram. You showed me a Rameswaram right here. Even though I could not go to the World Tamil Conference, the feelings of self-respect appropriate to my going there were contained here; just as Rama created a Rameswaram for the sparrow, you said from hearts pouring out affection that you had created a World Tamil Nadu here for me, and you called this the World Tamil Conference.

Every person's heart is itself a world. In every person's heart there is a world.
