## Source-sensitive translation choices

- the TOC exclamation mark is documented but is not added to the English heading, following the actual story-opening witness.
- `மிசாக் கைதிகள்` is rendered as **MISA detainees** without adding outside Emergency-era exposition.
- `உடன்பிறப்பே` is retained as **Udanpirappe** inside the embedded letter rather than forced into a single English kinship/political gloss.
- corrected readings including `தூக்கி நிறுத்திய`, `கடிதமாகத் தீட்டினேன்`, `தொடுவான்! துவளமாட்டான்.`, `சல்லாபத்`, `தொடங்குவதற்கு`, and `யாரையாவது, உதவிக்கு அழைக்கலாம் என்றாலோ` are translated from the current verified Tamil; earlier rejected readings are not reintroduced.
- dense/archaic literary wording on scans 49–53 is translated conservatively from this controlling edition. No outside Yasodhara Kaviyam or Kuruntokai edition is used to rewrite the Tamil or supply a conventional English version.
- `அம்ச தாளிகா மஞ்சம்` is retained conservatively as **Amsa Thalika couch** rather than assigned an unverified material/cultural identification.
- `எருக்கம்பூ வார்த்தைகள்` is rendered literally as **erukkam-flower words** rather than silently replacing the image with an editorial characterization.
- the Kuruntokai lines are translated directly from the immediate Tamil witness and its explanatory prose. Source-visible forms `வெல் அறை` / `கையில` remain untouched in Tamil; the English rendering does not claim to be a received or numbered scholarly translation.
- place-like/punning `மணப் பாறையா? வாள்பாறையா?` is retained as **Manapparai? Vaalpaarai?** rather than decomposed into speculative literal meanings.
