## Difficult / source-sensitive choices

The complete `POSSIBLE_ERRORS_FOR_REVIEW.md` queue was read before translation. No queued Tamil form was silently corrected.

- `தமிழகமுழுதும்`, `அங்கிங்கெனாதபடி`, `அன்றாடங்காய்ச்சிகளான`, and other joined source forms are translated by their verified contextual meaning; the Tamil remains untouched.
- `புலித்தைலம்` is retained as **Pulithailam**, avoiding an unsupported product identification.
- Karuppi's colloquial `அங்கணபோயி`, `ரெண்டு மணிக்கே கீழே`, `தபா`, and related speech are rendered as conversational English rather than regularising the Tamil layer.
- `காவி வைத்த காக்கி நிறப்பனியன்` is translated closely as a **khaki-coloured vest dyed with ochre**; no lexical correction was made.
- the Kannagi/Madhavi/Silappathikaram wordplay is preserved through **anklet / Kannagi / Madhavi / Kaanal Vari**; `Kaanal Vari` is transliterated rather than externally glossed inside the story.
- the tax/`வரி` pun in the Leela exchange is preserved as far as natural English permits by moving directly from *Kaanal Vari* to “Don't talk about tax.”
- `ஒளிப்பிரவாகம்`, the long ellipsis after `காவல் நிலையத்தில்.....`, and cut-out terminology remain structurally faithful without silently altering Tamil punctuation.
- the Bharathidasan and Bharathi passages are translated directly from the canonical Tamil and retain their line breaks; no published outside translation was imported.
- `சுலபத்தில் விடாத மணலில்` is rendered as sand that **would not easily release a foot once it sank in**.
- `பத்தாம்பசலிக் கட்டுப்பெட்டிக்` is rendered as a **mouldy, hidebound** imagination, documenting rather than normalising the unusual Tamil expression.
- `தரையிலிட்ட மீன்` is rendered as the **fish laid on the ground**, following the source-confirmed reading.
- `கரையைவிட்டகன்று` is rendered as **leaving the wave-beaten shore**.
- the vendor call `கண்டல் முறுக்குக் கடலை` is kept partly transliterated as **“Kandal murukku, peanuts!”** because the first item/source form is distinctive and the source does not justify replacing it with a guessed commodity name.
- colloquial Sivakasi dialogue (`பாத்தோம்`, `திடீர்னு`, `செத்துட்டாரு`, `பொழைச்சிக்கிட்டாங்க`, `பஸ்ஸில அடிபட்டு`) is rendered naturally while preserving the underlying events and register.
- `நம்மாலியன்ற` is represented contextually as **whatever help is within our power**; the Tamil remains in its source-close form.
- five closing ornaments are retained as **◉ ◉ ◉ ◉ ◉**.

## Structural / rhetorical review

- the opening contrast between a statewide economic shutdown and the writer's cable-TV inconvenience is preserved as satire;
- the playful literary references in the domestic scene are retained without explanatory footnotes in the story body;
- Ekalivan's repeated search for a socially meaningful short-story theme remains a sequence of formed-and-rejected ideas;
- the story deliberately rejects both a recycled police-station Kannagi theme and a conservative crab/girl analogy before arriving at lived poverty;
- the fish episode retains Ekalivan's worry that a political story may be difficult to publish;
- the Sivakasi boy's account displaces Ekalivan's manufactured story ideas with an unfinished real tragedy;
- the mother's sudden death interrupts the boy's “story,” and the final request for snacks from the fashionable couple returns the writer to the commodity he had bought while leaving him motionless like a statue;
- no outside literary, political, labour-law or historical explanation was inserted into the English story body.
