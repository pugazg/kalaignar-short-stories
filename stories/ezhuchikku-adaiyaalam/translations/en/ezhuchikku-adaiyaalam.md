We have read the story of Abhimanyu in the Bharatham. He was a young man, a married young man. Even though that young man died in the Bharatha war, his

valour did not disappear. The reason is that he broke through the chakra formation that no one could break. Abhimanyu broke through that chakra formation which even Arjuna could not break. Therefore, although Bhima's valour is praised in the Bharatha war, although Parthiban's valour is praised, although Gandiban's valour is praised, and although Ashwatthaman's valour is praised, the valour of that young man Abhimanyu is praised one step above all of them. He lost an arm. After losing the arm, he clenched a sword in his mouth and fought. Then his legs were severed. He rolled across the field as a trunk and fought. Finally, the story says that the schemer Jayadrathan killed him from behind. Thus Abhimanyu remained a warrior until the very end and died. This is a sign of what the rising spirit of youth was like in the age of the puranas.
