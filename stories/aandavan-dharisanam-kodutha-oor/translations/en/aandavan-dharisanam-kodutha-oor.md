# The Village Where God Gave Darshan

<!-- source scan 18; printed page 17 -->

“...Was it not at Ambal Thirumariththile itself... that Sundaramurthi Nayanar performed a great yajna? In that yajna, Brahmins learned in the Vedas served as priests and chanted mantras. At that time, Thyagaraja Peruman came from Tiruvarur, he and his consort together, disguised as ‘Pulayars’, carrying a pot of liquor and bearing a dead calf on his shoulder, and came to that yajna,” says the *sthala puranam*. Is this not the town where God himself came as a person of a downtrodden caste and granted darshan!
