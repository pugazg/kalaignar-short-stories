## Difficult / source-sensitive choices

- `நரியூர் நந்தியப்பன்` is rendered **“Nandiyappan of Nariyur.”** This deliberately mirrors, rather than merges with, Story 4's reversed pair `நந்தியூர் நரியப்பன்` / **“Nariyappan of Nandiyur.”** The two stories remain distinct.
- `நந்தியூர் பஞ்சாயத்துக்கார நரியப்பன்` is rendered as **“Nariyappan, panchayat man of Nandiyur”** without assigning a more specific modern office-title.
- `குபேர சம்பத்து`, `வைடூரியம்`, `ஆலவட்டம்`, and `தர்பார் மண்டபம்` are translated conservatively as **Kubera-like wealth**, **vaiduryam gems**, **ceremonial fan**, and **durbar hall**. No external historical identification is added.
- Source-odd scan-55 `காணத்தவங்கிடப்பர்`, `மறைவ துண்டோ?`, and `எண்ணிய துண்டு` are translated by their immediate contextual sense only; the verified Tamil remains unchanged.
- Source-odd scan-56 `தெய்வானு கூலம்` is rendered minimally as **“divine favour”**; `சிறப்புக் களையும்`, `உணர்ந்தே யிருப்பீர்கள்`, `என்பாடு`, and `அஃதே` are likewise translated contextually without normalizing the Tamil.
- The recurring `நாட்டாண்மைக்காரர்கள்` / `ஒப்பக்காரர்` pair is kept consistent with Story 4 as **“administrators”** / **“assenter.”** This preserves the two-letter satire without imposing an external constitutional identification.
- Madhavi and Kannagi are retained as names in the stage-role comparison. No explanation beyond what the verified Tamil supplies is inserted.
- Scan 57 `சடுகுடுபோல்` is retained as **“like a game of sadugudu”** rather than silently replacing the source term with a modern sports label.
- The film title `பராசக்தி` is retained as ***Parasakthi***, and `குணசேகரன்` as **Gunasekaran**. The translation does not add film-history information from outside the story.
- Verified source-odd `தங்கள் முடங்கள்` is rendered minimally as **“your message”** from the immediate letter context. This is an English translation choice only; the Tamil form is not altered or reclassified.
- `பொன் வைத்த இடத்தில் பூ வைத்தது போல` is translated literally as **“placing a flower where gold belongs,”** preserving the contrast that immediately leads to `பொன் பூ! புகழ்ப் பூ!`.
- Scan 58 `பந்து மித்திரர்களுடன்`, `ஷேத்திரங்களை`, and `பகவத் அனுக்கிரகத்தை` are rendered as **relatives and friends**, **sacred kshetras**, and **divine grace**; no specific pilgrimage sites or theological interpretation are invented.
- Source-odd `மட்டுந்தானு?` is rendered by its clear interrogative sense, and `பத்தோடு பதினொன்று` is kept close to the source as **“the eleventh among ten.”**

## Structural / rhetorical review

**PASS.** The English preserves the reply-letter form, exaggerated praise of Nandiyappan's former grandeur, the speaker's own compromised panchayat role, the Madhavi/Kannagi double-role analogy, the *Parasakthi* king-and-minister self-dialogue, the decision to move fully to the administrators' side, and the closing satire that one more unnecessary office may as well survive for people like him to enjoy.
