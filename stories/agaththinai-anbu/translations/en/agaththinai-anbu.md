# Love Within!

> **English translation of the verified Tamil archival reading.** Controlling source: 1987 collection `கலைஞர் சொன்ன குட்டிக் கதைகள்`, lower scan **45 / printed page 44 → upper scan 46 / printed page 45**.

<!-- anthology scan: 45; printed page: 44; source span: lower -->

A beautiful woman. There was no one in this world whose beauty equalled hers; the kind of beauty described by mythologists! Not even Urvashi, Rambha or Tilottama. Such was the beauty of that woman. She went to bathe in a cool-water stream. As she finished bathing and was coming up onto the bank, another woman arrived with a baby in her arms and a pot in her hand.

The woman who arrived seated the baby on a raised spot and began scrubbing the pot in the stream.

At that moment, the child on the raised spot began crawling forward and was in danger of falling into the water below.

<!-- anthology scan: 46; printed page: 45; source span: upper -->

Could the mother run and stop the child from falling? She could not, because she was so far away. So she looked at that beautiful woman and said, “Run and save the child.”

But the beautiful woman looked at it indifferently and went away.

Before the mother could run over, the child fell into the water. Even though the child fell, because the mother went at once and lifted the child out, she was able to give the child the breath of life.

Then the mother looked at that beautiful woman and said:

“What can all the outer limbs do for the body
of those who lack love, the inner limb?”

“What is the use of being so beautiful, girl! There is no love within you,” she said.

## Translation note

The title `அகத்திணை அன்பு!` is rendered **Love Within!** to carry the story's contrast between outward beauty and inward love. The quoted two-line couplet is translated from the verified wording in this 1987 witness rather than silently regularized.
