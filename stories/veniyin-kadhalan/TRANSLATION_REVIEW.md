## Sensitive-content handling

The story contains sexual coercion, military violence, contemplated poisoning, death and a final fall causing severe head injury. The translation does not add sexual or violent mechanics beyond the source, does not soften Veni’s accusation against Gundappan, and preserves Surya’s moral conflict and rejection of murder as the source presents them.

## Structural and rhetorical review

- Surya’s opening prayer establishes that she already loves an unnamed man before Veni’s relationship to Kandan is revealed;
- Gundappan’s instruction to guard Veni comes before Veni’s accusation, preserving the story’s reversal;
- Surya’s own past with Kandan is revealed only after Veni’s account;
- Surya’s brief murderous thought is followed immediately by the nursing-profession moral check and her decision that Veni must live;
- the final trolley scene resolves both women’s hope at once and remains the story ending without added explanation.
