- `சூர்யா`, `வேணி`, `குண்டப்பன்`, `கந்தன்`, and `தாமோதர்` are rendered **Surya, Veni, Gundappan, Kandan**, and **Damodar**.
- source-derived hospital/register language such as `பென்வார்டில்`, `சார்ஜண்ட்`, `‘பெட்’`, and `‘பினிஷ்’` is rendered conservatively as **women’s ward**, **sergeant**, quoted **“bed”**, and quoted **“finished”**.
- `வைப்பாட்டி` is translated as **kept woman**, preserving the accusation’s social meaning without changing the Tamil.
- `புண்ணகிப்போன உடல்கள்` is rendered minimally as bodies ruined by wounds rather than silently correcting the source form.
- `கூண்டுக் கிளி ஆக்குவேனென்றான்` becomes **he said he would make me a caged bird**, following the source-corrected canonical Tamil.
- unusual `தரித்திர நாராயணனும் கந்தனுக்கு` is translated by immediate class/poverty contrast without rewriting the Tamil.
- `ஹைகோர்ட்டின் படிக்கட்டுகளைக் கட்டுப்பதிலேயே` is kept source-close as the father spending the wealth “on the steps of the High Court,” without importing a legal-history explanation.
- `கந்தர்வ லோகத்திலே` remains **Gandharva world**.
- `கந்தனு?` and `வாழ்க்கைத் துண்டித்த` are translated from immediate emotional sense while their canonical Tamil forms remain unchanged.

## Sensitive-content handling

The story contains sexual coercion, military violence, contemplated poisoning, death and a final fall causing severe head injury. The translation does not add sexual or violent mechanics beyond the source, does not soften Veni’s accusation against Gundappan, and preserves Surya’s moral conflict and rejection of murder as the source presents them.

## Structural and rhetorical review

- Surya’s opening prayer establishes that she already loves an unnamed man before Veni’s relationship to Kandan is revealed;
- Gundappan’s instruction to guard Veni comes before Veni’s accusation, preserving the story’s reversal;
- Surya’s own past with Kandan is revealed only after Veni’s account;
- Surya’s brief murderous thought is followed immediately by the nursing-profession moral check and her decision that Veni must live;
- the final trolley scene resolves both women’s hope at once and remains the story ending without added explanation.
