## Source-review method

All six story scans were directly reviewed from high-resolution source renders. Enlarged full-span crops were used for older Tamil glyph forms, unusual wording, punctuation, spacing and physical joins. Scan **231** was independently inspected as the Story 34 boundary witness. The controlling scan, not contextual expectation, determined the readings.

Older Tamil glyph shapes were checked against their complete word and sentence spans. In particular, `றா`-bearing forms such as `வரவேற்றாள்` and `எப்படியிருக்கிறாள்` were not interpreted from modern-font appearance alone.

During the final full-span pass, scan **228** was reopened and the provisional reading `கூண்டுக் கிளி ஆகுவேனென்றான்` was corrected to the visibly printed **`கூண்டுக் கிளி ஆக்குவேனென்றான்`**. The corrected reading is used in the page record, Tamil assembly, source metadata, review queue and this audit.
