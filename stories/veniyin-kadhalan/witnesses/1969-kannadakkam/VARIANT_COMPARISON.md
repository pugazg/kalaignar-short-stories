# Variant comparison — வேணியின் காதலன் — 1969 witness ↔ 1977 canonical

## Authority and method

The attached **1969 second edition** is an independent comparison witness only. The **1977 first-edition anthology** remains canonical.

The complete 1969 story, scans **25–31 / printed 24–30**, was compared end-to-end against the verified 1977 canonical assembly and page records. Historical glyph identity was read as character identity rather than modern visual resemblance. Routine line-wrap differences are not textual variants.

No OCR or external edition controls this comparison.

## Orthography / spacing / punctuation classes

Recurring edition-level differences include:

- `லயித்துப்போய்` ↔ canonical `லயித்துப் போய்`;
- `ஏற்றுக்கொள்ளத் தான்` ↔ `ஏற்றுக்கொள்ளத்தான்`;
- `அவ்வளவு தான்` ↔ `அவ்வளவுதான்`;
- different dashes / commas around Gundappan–Surya dialogue;
- `புதுப்பித்துக் கொள்ள` ↔ `புதுப்பித்துக்கொள்ள`;
- different joining around `மறந்து விடுவான்`, `பிடித்துக் கொள்ளலாம்`, `இறுகிப் போன`, `மின் வேகத்`, and `கருத்துப் போய்`;
- 1969 `நான் கூட சிறிது நேரத்தில்` ↔ canonical `நான்கூடச் சிறிது நேரத்தில்`;
- 1969 `யிருக்கிறார்` ↔ canonical `இருக்கிறார்`;
- numerous ellipsis and quotation-mark differences.

None is silently normalized into the canonical layer.

## Canonical confirmations

The witness provides useful independent support for:

- `கூண்டுக் கிளி ஆக்குவேனென்றான்`;
- unusual `கந்தனு?`;
- the full Veni / Kandan / Gundappan narrative sequence;
- the same terminal death scene.

It does **not** support the 1977 source-specific spacing `யோசனை தான்!`; the 1969 witness has `யோசனைதான்!`. That difference is retained as evidence of edition change, while the verified 1977 scan remains authoritative for its own canonical text.
