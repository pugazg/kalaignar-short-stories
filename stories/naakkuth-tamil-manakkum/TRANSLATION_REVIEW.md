- `கரித்துண்டை` is followed as the verified source and rendered as **piece of charcoal**.
- The unusual closing source phrase `உற்சாக மேமிட்டால்` is not altered in Tamil; English uses the least-speculative contextual rendering **“raising resounding cries of enthusiasm.”**
- `அண்ணா` and `கழகம்` remain **Anna** and **Kazhagam** without outside explanatory material.
