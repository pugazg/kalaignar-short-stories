# The Elder's Judgment

<!-- source scan 34; printed page 33 -->

Karikaar Peruvallathan ‘when he was a very young boy had to assume the responsibility of ruling the country. To him

<!-- source scan 35; printed page 34 -->

came two parties to present their petitions. Would both sides accept the judgment delivered by that youth seated in the court? Would the members of the assembly accept it? Because of this doubt, the young Karikaar Peruvallathan put on a beard and moustache white as a crane and sat in the court to deliver judgment.

Thinking, “Such an old man is giving the judgment; it must surely be a good judgment,” they accepted the decision and went away.
