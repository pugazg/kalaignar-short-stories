## Source-sensitive choices

- The quoted `எடுக்கவோ கோக்கவோ?` is rendered **“Shall I pick them up or string them?”** so the semantic force of the title phrase remains clear while the heading itself stays transliterated.
- Source `சோதரைப் போர்க்களத்தில்` is not silently repaired from epic context. The uncertain lexical form is conservatively represented as **“Sotharai”** and is not identified from outside knowledge.
- `வில்லிப்புத்தூரார் பாரதம்` is rendered as **Villiputhurar's Bharatham**, preserving the source attribution without importing another edition or conventional English title.
- The TOC exclamation mark absent from the opening heading remains a documented source variance rather than being normalized.
