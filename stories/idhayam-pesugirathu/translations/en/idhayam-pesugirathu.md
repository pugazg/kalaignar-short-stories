# The Heart Speaks

I read an English story. In that story, a mother had a son. That son had a beloved. The beloved handed her lover a knife and said, “Bring me your mother's heart.” “My dear! I will not disobey your command. This very moment I will go and bring you my mother's heart,” he said, and left with the knife. The handsome young man went to make an offering to his beloved,

and when the mother saw her son she called to him, “Come, son, let us eat.” But the son lunged at his mother with the knife and stabbed her. He split open her chest and took out her heart. Carrying it, he ran to place it at his beloved's feet. In the speed of his running, he stumbled and fell.

Then his mother's heart, which was in his hand, asked, “What is it, son! Did you get hurt? Does it pain?”

Only a mother has such a special quality. We have Tamil as our mother tongue. It is to protect that Tamil that we struggle.
