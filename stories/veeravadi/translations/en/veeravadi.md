# Veeravadi

<!-- source scan 18; printed page 17 -->

In the days of kings, the village granted as an *inam* to the people of the community called ‘Nokkar’ came to be known as Veeravadi. It is said that an old-time king himself gave that hamlet this name.

People belonging to the Nokkar community would tie *muram* — winnowing trays — to their backs, learn the art of flying, and display it with great skill. One day they approached the king and asked for an opportunity to demonstrate their ability. The king, unable to believe them, said, “Tie the muram to yourselves and fly. Whichever village you land in, I am ready to grant that entire village as an inam to your community.”

Accepting that challenge, the Nokkar people tied on the muram, flew, and landed in a village. The delighted king granted that very village to them
