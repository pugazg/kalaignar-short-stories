# English E3 Terminology / Cultural-Consistency Conventions — 1977 Anthology

This is the durable cross-anthology convention record for **E3** of the 1977 English post-Tamil re-audit.

## Authority

1. live `main`;
2. final canonical Tamil;
3. story-local Tamil / English audit records;
4. established English treatment already supported by the source.

This ledger does **not** authorize outside normalization. A story-specific source distinction always overrides mechanical uniformity.

## Conventions established through Stories 1–13

### Names and titles

- Preserve the story-established spelling of personal names unless the same person/source form is demonstrably inconsistent.
- `முதலியார்` title/name component → **Mudaliar**.
- `சுவாமி` as title → **Swami**.
- `மடம்` as the religious institution → **mutt**.

### Recurring religious / mythological names

- `சிவன்` → **Siva**.
- `பார்வதி` → **Parvati**.
- `முருகன்` → **Murugan**; direct vocative `முருகா` may remain **Muruga**.
- `கைலாசம்` → **Kailasa**.
- the established mythological identification `திரெளபதி` → **Draupadi** where the story itself supports that identification.
- `விபூதி` → **sacred ash** when used generically.

### Kinship / address

- Culturally marked direct address may remain transliterated: **Amma, Appa, Ayya, Aththaan, Thambi**.
- Ordinary narrative kinship may be translated naturally as **mother, father, brother, brother-in-law**, etc.
- Do not force a single English form across narratorial and vocative uses when the source register differs.
- recurring exclamation `அய்யோ` → **Ayyo**.

### Clothing / cultural vocabulary

- source `வேட்டி` → **veshti**.
- `சேலை` in ordinary clothing context → **sari** where already established.
- `தீபாவளி` → **Deepavali**.
- `புறநானூறு` → **Purananuru**.

### Source-odd / opaque forms

Verified but lexically unusual forms remain conservative when a stronger identification would require outside knowledge. Examples already accepted include **Pideldō**, **Nayanavalli-thathai**, **thoorattram**, **nishta**, **Namap-Parvati Padē**, **mūḍātmā / jñānātmā**, **ammammi**, **pusvaanam**, and **mudangal**.

E3 may improve cross-story consistency, but must not convert such forms into guessed modern equivalents.
